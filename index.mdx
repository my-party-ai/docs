Fast Party is an event planning and management platform for everything from a family dinner to a 500-person conference, a college alumni network or a club with thirty sections. It brings the people, plans, messages and activity around an event into one place, so everyone knows what's happening and you stay in control.

**Plan online. Live offline.**

## How Fast Party fits together

Everything in Fast Party is built from three layers. Understand these and the rest of this guide will make sense quickly.
<CardGroup cols={3}>
<Card title="Events" icon="calendar">
    A single occasion: a birthday, a reunion, a conference. You set it up on the Event Page and run it from the Event Dashboard.
</Card>
<Card title="Circles" icon="circle-nodes">
    A saved group of people you plan with again and again. Members first, events follow.
</Card>
<Card title="Organizations" icon="building">
  The home for an institution, club or business. One roster, many circles, every event, under your own brand.
</Card>
</CardGroup>

An organization holds circles, and circles hold events. You can also create events on their own without a circle or an organization. [Read how it all fits together →](/get-started/how-it-works)

## Two ways to work

**In the app.** Use Fast Party on the web at [app.fastparty.ai](https://app.fastparty.ai) or on iOS and Android. The **Ask Antsy** button on every screen shows nudges for your events and short help videos. [Get the app →](/platforms)

**With your own AI.** Connect Fast Party to Claude or ChatGPT once, then create events, invite guests, build schedules and ask questions about your data in plain language. [Connect your AI →](/platforms#connect-your-ai)
