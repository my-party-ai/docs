Every event comes with a set of **event tools**. They're optional: you set them up on the Event Page for your guests, and switch on only what the occasion needs. Guests use them in the browser from their invitation link, with no app to download.

They fall into four groups, by what they're for.

## Things you put together for guests to read

These make your invitation worth opening. Someone deciding whether to come wants to know what actually happens.

| Tool | What it does | Worth it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**Event Schedule**](/private-events/event-schedule) | The running order, so guests know what happens when. Single or multi-day, with files and links on each activity. | There's more than one thing happening |
| [**Event Lineup**](/private-events/event-lineup) | Who's speaking or performing, and when, linked to their slot on the schedule. | People are coming partly to see someone |
| [**Event Folder**](/private-events/event-folder) | Files such as menus, maps, tickets or a dress code, shared with every guest or only the ones you pick. | There's something guests need to read beforehand |

## Things you ask your guests

These appear when guests RSVP or register, so it's best to switch them on **before** you invite. A guest who doesn't know their answer yet still learns you want it, and comes back when they do.

| Tool | What it does | Worth it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**Guest Preferences**](/private-events/food-beverage) | What guests would like to eat and drink, and any dietary needs, so you can plan the menu. | You're feeding people and someone needs numbers |
| [**Travel & Stay**](/private-events/travel-stay) | Guests' arrival, room and departure details, so you can plan pickups and rooms. | People are coming from out of town or staying over |
| [**Gifting & Best Wishes**](/private-events/gifting) | A way for guests to send gifts and heartfelt messages. | People will want to give something |
| [**Potluck Manager**](/private-events/potluck) | A sign-up sheet for who brings what, with no duplicates and no gaps. | Your guests bring the food, not the venue |

## For guests on the day, and after

These belong to the day itself and to afterwards.

| Tool | What it does | Worth it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**Check-In**](/private-events/self-check-in) | Guests check themselves in when they arrive, so you know who made it. At a Registration Event, attendees are checked in by [QR code](/public-events/registrant-check-in) instead. | You need a real count of who came |
| [**Real-Time Guest ETA**](/private-events/guest-eta) | Guests share their location on the way, so you see when they'll arrive. | You're timing when the food goes out or the surprise happens |
| [**Photos**](/private-events/photos) | The event's pictures, in one shared album. | Afterwards, always |
| [**Feedback Corner**](/private-events/feedback-corner) | A short feedback form built for your event, with a summary of what guests said. | You'll run this event again |

## For running the event

| Tool | What it does | Worth it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**Tasks**](/private-events/tasks) | Your event's to-do list, with due dates and people assigned. A guest you assign a task sees that task, not your whole list. | You're not doing this alone |

## Setting tools up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick what this event needs">
    A wedding usually needs Travel & Stay and Guest Preferences. A conference needs Event Schedule and Event Lineup. A dinner might need only Potluck Manager. You don't need all twelve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set each one up on the Event Page">
    Open the tool from the Event Page and fill it in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    Most tools have their own publish or share step. Guests see a tool only once you publish it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When to set them up

**Before you invite, if you can.** Everything you've set up arrives with the invitation, in one message, while people are already reading.

You can still add tools at any time. Guests are told automatically, but not immediately: tools you switch on after the invitation are collected into **one update**, sent the next day.

- **Set up several tools on the same day** and guests get a single update. Spread them across a week and guests get a message a day.
- **A tool that asks guests for something,** switched on within two days of the event, leaves them little time to answer.

<Tip>
  Let [your connected AI](/platforms#connect-your-ai) set tools up for you: "Set up the schedule for Saturday: welcome drinks at 7:30, dinner at 8, cake at 9:30, and publish it. Then create a potluck with mains, salads and desserts."
</Tip>
