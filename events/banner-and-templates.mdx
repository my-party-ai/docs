Before anyone reads a word about your event, they see its pictures. The **event template** is the invitation card people receive and share. The **banner** runs across the top of every page of your event. Together they set the mood, tell people at a glance what kind of event it is, and make your event instantly recognizable in a busy WhatsApp chat or circle feed.

Add both to every event. It takes a few minutes, and it's the difference between an event that looks like a form and one people are excited to open.

## Sizes to use

Use these sizes so your images look sharp and nothing important gets cropped.

| | Event template | Event banner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recommended size** | 1200 × 1745 px | 2731 × 1024 px |
| **Shape** | Portrait | Wide, 8:3 aspect ratio |
| **File format** | JPG or PNG | JPG or PNG |
| **Quality** | High resolution | High resolution |

## Add an event template

Click the pencil on the event template, when you create the event or any time after. You can:

- **Pick one from our template store.** Search, or browse by category: Gathering, Party, Celebration, Festival, Corporate, Meetup, Family, Night, Outdoors and Kids. You can also browse the full collection on the website's [Celebration Hub](https://www.fastparty.ai/celebration-hub).
- **Upload From Device** to use your own design.

### Multi-day events: a template for each day

**Every event can be a multi-day event**, whether it's an RSVP Event, a Registration Event or an Open Event. Each day can have its **own invitation template**, up to **5 templates** per event, from the store, your own designs, or a mix.

That's ideal for a wedding with a Mehendi, Sangeet and reception, a three-day festival, or a conference with a different theme each day: guests get a card that matches the day they're invited to.

## Add a banner

On the Event Page, click **Add banner** in the top-right corner of the banner area. You can:

- **Upload your own** image
- **Generate one with AI** right in Fast Party

## Tips for great event images

- **Use the recommended sizes.** Smaller images look blurry on large screens, and other shapes get cropped.
- **Match the occasion.** A reunion, a club night and a wedding should each feel like themselves at a glance.
- **Add both.** The template is what gets shared, and the banner is what greets people once they arrive on your event.
