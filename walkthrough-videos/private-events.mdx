## What's covered

- Creating a private (Regular Event) and filling in event details
- Adding guests manually, by bulk entry, CSV import, and from a Circle
- Sending invitations and tracking RSVPs
- Building and publishing the event schedule (agenda)
- Setting up food and beverage preferences
- Creating a potluck sign-up with categories and slots
- Adding tasks and assigning them to co-hosts
- Using the Event Folder to share documents with guests
- Guest ETA and live location tracking on the day
- Check-in — manual, self-check-in, and QR code
- Photos and the activity feed
- Gifting and Best Wishes
