Tasks keeps track of everything that has to happen, from booking the caterer to printing passes, with an owner and a date on each. Antsy follows up on the overdue ones.

## Create a task

Open **Tasks** from the Event Page and click **Create Task**.

  <img
    src="/images/ev-tasks-create.png"

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Task Name** | Required, up to 30 characters |
| **Priority** | Required, such as Medium or High |
| **Labels** | Optional, to group related tasks |
| **Assignee** | One person who owns the task |
| **Collaborators** | Any number of people who help |
| **Due Date** | Date and time |
| **Task Description** | Optional, up to 280 characters |
| **Attachments** | Optional, up to 5 files |

## Who you can assign

Assign tasks to hosts, co-hosts and guests. They're notified with the task and its due date, and a guest sees only the tasks assigned to them, never your whole list.

A **collaborator** can also be someone outside the event, such as a driver or a decorator, who needs to see and act on one task only. They don't join the guest list and can't see anything else.

## See what's left

- **Board** shows task cards, grouped by who they're assigned to.
- **Calendar** shows tasks on their due dates.
- **Search** by task, assignee or label, and use **Filters** to focus on what matters now.

The Event Dashboard shows how many tasks are in progress and completed, and counts it towards the event's health score.
