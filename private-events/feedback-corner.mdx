The Feedback Corner is a post-event feature that lets you collect structured feedback from guests after the event is over — ratings, comments, and overall impressions in one place.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Host enables Feedback Corner">
    After the event, the host turns on the Feedback Corner from the event settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Guests receive a feedback request">
    Guests are notified and invited to submit their feedback via their invitation link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Host reviews results">
    The host sees aggregate ratings and all written comments in one dashboard view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What guests can submit

- An overall event rating
- Written comments or suggestions

## What hosts see

- Aggregate rating across all responses
- Individual written comments from guests
- Response count

<Tip>
  Use Feedback Corner after milestone events — weddings, corporate retreats, conferences — to understand what worked and what to improve next time.
</Tip>
