The Event Schedule is your event's running order. Guests see what happens when, add activities to their own calendar, and always see the latest version. Update it once and everyone sees the change.

## Add activities

Open **Event Schedule** from the Event Page and click **Add Activity**. Each activity has:

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Title** | What's happening, such as "Welcome drinks" or "Club Parade" |
| **Start and end time** | When it begins and ends |
| **Notes** | Optional instructions, such as "Main gate. Carry your registration QR." |
| **Location or room** | Optional, useful when things happen in different places |
| **Links** | Optional, such as a menu, a map or a stream |
| **Attachments** | Optional, up to 5 files per activity |

Activities are sorted by time. For a **multi-day event**, they're grouped by date, and the Event Dashboard can filter to a single day.

## Publish it

Your schedule is private until you click **Publish Schedule**. After that, guests see it from their invitation link, with no account needed. Edits you make later show up for guests straight away.

From the top of the page you can also **share** the schedule and **download** it as a PDF for printing.

## On each activity

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Guest Check-In** | Check people in to this activity, for example a workshop or a dinner with limited seats. |
| **Add to Calendar** | Guests add the activity to their own calendar. |

The dashboard's **Timeline** tab shows what's up next and how many are checked in to each activity.

## Link your lineup

Speakers and performers in your [Event Lineup](/private-events/event-lineup) can be linked to activities, so guests see who's on in each slot.
