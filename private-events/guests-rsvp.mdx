## Invite guests

There are two ways in, and many events use both.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Add Guest" icon="user-plus">
    Add people by phone number or email, pick them from people you've invited before, or **Upload CSV** with names, emails and phone numbers. Each guest gets a **personal link** and arrives already recognized.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Share Invite" icon="share-nodes">
    Share one invite link on WhatsApp, email or social media. Anyone with the link can reply. Best when you don't know everyone who's coming.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For an event in a circle, every member is invited when you make the event live. You can still add people outside the circle afterwards. [Events in a circle →](/circles/circle-events)

## What guests receive

Guests invited by phone number get their invitation on **WhatsApp**, and guests invited by email get it by **email**. It includes the event name, date, time, venue, who's hosting, and their personal RSVP link. When they reply, they get a confirmation. [How notifications work →](/notifications/overview)

<Note>
  Guests don't need the Fast Party app to RSVP. They tap the link and reply in their browser.
</Note>

## Track replies

Filter the list to see exactly who you're waiting on:

| Filter | Who's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Everyone invited |
| **Circle Member** / **Non-Circle Member** | For circle events: members of the circle, and people you added from outside it |
| **Yes** | Coming |
| **No** | Can't make it |
| **Maybe** | Not sure yet |
| **Pending** | Invited, no reply yet |

Guests who say Yes or Maybe tell you how many are coming, so each name shows a headcount such as **2 Adults** or **4 Adults, 2 Kids**.

## Manage a guest

Open the **⋮** menu next to a guest to edit their details, make them a co-host, or remove them from the event.

The totals, and a reminder for anyone who hasn't replied, are on the [Event Dashboard](/private-events/event-dashboard).
