Gifting & Best Wishes lets guests send a gift, contribute to a Gift Package, shop your registry, or leave a heartfelt wish — even guests who can't come. No separate app or payment link needed, and money goes straight to you. Fast Party never holds it.

## Enabling gifts

Gifts are off by default. Open your event, go to **Gifting & Best Wishes**, and turn on **Enable Gifts & Best Wishes**. Then set up the ways guests can give, and tap **Share** to show them on your event page.

## Digital Gifting

Digital Gifting has three tabs: **UPI QR**, **Gift Package** and **Registry**.

### UPI QR

Add up to 3 UPI QR codes or UPI IDs so guests can send you money directly. Typing the UPI ID works best — photos of QR codes don't always scan.

Guests pay you through their own UPI app and can upload a screenshot of the payment. You'll see every gift in **Summary**.

### Gift Package

A Gift Package lets guests contribute toward something bigger, like a honeymoon or a new appliance. You can create up to 5.

Each Gift Package works in one of two ways:

|  | How it works | What guests can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One price** | You set a single amount for the whole package. | Gift the whole amount, or chip in what they like. |
| **Broken into parts** | You list the parts, each with its own price — for example flights, hotel and a dinner. | Pick one part, then gift it in full or chip in toward it. |

A package is either one price or broken into parts, not both. When it's broken into parts, guests choose a part — they can't pay for the whole package in one go. If you want guests to be able to gift the whole thing, create it as a single package with one price.

<Note>
  Gift Packages are paid to your UPI, so add a UPI method first. When you create a package, choose which UPI account receives the money for it.
</Note>

Guests pay you directly by UPI. Fast Party tracks each guest's contribution against the Gift Package, and you can confirm each one, or mark it as not received, in **Summary**.

### Registry

Add up to 3 registry or wish-list links so guests can choose and buy gifts directly from the store. Links from Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Target, Zola, Crate & Barrel, Williams Sonoma, The Knot and MyRegistry are supported.

Give each link a label guests will see, like "Our Amazon registry". Guests open the link, buy on the store, and the store handles delivery. Registry purchases don't show in your Summary.

### Coming soon

Gift cards and physical gifting from trusted partners are on the way.

## Summary

**Summary** shows every UPI gift and Gift Package contribution in one place. Only you see who gave and how much.

## Best Wishes

Best Wishes lets guests send a personal message — a note, a memory, or a warm wish — attached to your event.

**For guests:**
- Write a message up to **2,000 characters**.
- Optionally attach up to **2 media files** (photos or videos).
- Submit from their invitation link.

**For hosts:**
- Customize the **banner text** shown on the Best Wishes page — set a personal prompt or a themed heading.
- View all submitted wishes in one place from the event dashboard.

<Tip>
  Best Wishes is a lovely alternative to a guestbook at weddings, milestone birthdays, and retirement parties. Guests can submit their wishes before, during, or after the event.
</Tip>

## Disabling gifts

You can turn gifting off at any time. If you turn it off after guests have already sent gifts or wishes, you can still see them, but new ones are no longer accepted.
