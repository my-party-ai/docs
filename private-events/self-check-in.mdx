## Turn it on

Open **Check-In** from the Event Page and switch on **Enable Check-In**. It's off by default and entirely optional. Turn it on the day before, so it's ready when people arrive.

## How it works

- **At an RSVP Event,** guests check themselves in when they arrive, from their invitation link. There's nothing for you to do at the door.
- **At a Registration Event,** attendees show the QR code on their ticket, and you or your team scan it. [Attendee Check-In →](/public-events/registrant-check-in)

Either way, the Check-In page counts **adults** and **kids** checked in, and lists who has arrived. The Event Dashboard shows the same count.

## Check in to one activity

For a multi-session event, you can also check people in to a single activity from the [Event Schedule](/private-events/event-schedule), such as a workshop or a dinner with limited seats.

## When to use it

* A larger gathering where you can't see at a glance who's in the room
* A moment you're timing, such as a toast, a surprise or dinner service, where you want everyone there first
* Any time you want a record of who came

For a small, informal get-together you may not need it at all, which is why it's optional.
