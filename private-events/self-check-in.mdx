Fast Party gives you multiple ways to track arrivals at your private event. You can check guests in manually, let guests self-check-in from their invitation link, or display a QR code at the door.

## Host manual check-in

As the host or co-host, you can check in guests one by one from the **Guest Check-In** page:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Guest Check-In">
    Go to your event and open the **Guest Check-In** view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the guest">
    Search by name or scroll through the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as arrived">
    Tap the check-in button next to their name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The guest list updates in real time so you can see at a glance who has arrived and who hasn't.

## Guest self-check-in

Guests can check themselves in when they arrive. From their invitation link, they tap **Check In** to confirm they've arrived. The host's check-in view updates automatically.

## QR code check-in

For faster check-in at the door, display a QR code:

- The QR code is available from the **Agenda** page in the Guest Check-In modal.
- Display it at the entrance — on a screen, a printed sign, or a tablet.
- Guests scan the code with their phone camera to check themselves in instantly.

<Tip>
  QR code check-in works well for events with a steady stream of arrivals — you don't need a person at the door manually checking off names.
</Tip>

## Check-in from the agenda

The Guest Check-In view is also accessible from the **Agenda** page. This is useful if you're managing the schedule and arrivals from the same screen during your event.
