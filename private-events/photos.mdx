Every event gets one album. As the host, you upload the photos and your guests see them all in one place, so nobody has to ask around for pictures later. Open **Photos** from the Event Page and click **Upload** to add them.

<Note>
  Only the host uploads event photos. Guests can view them, but can't upload.
</Note>

## Photo albums

Photos are organized into albums. Browse by album type:

| Album type | What's here |
|------------|-------------|
| **Host Albums** | Albums created by the event host |
| **Favorites** | Photos you've marked as a favorite |
| **Deleted** | Recently deleted photos (trash) |

## Viewing photos

Open your event and go to the **Photos** tab to see all photos for that event, and filter by album type.

## Adding photos

Hosts tap **Add Photos** and select images from their device. The photos are added to the event album and guests can see them.

## Adding photos to Favorites

Tap the heart icon on any photo to add it to your **Favorites** album. Favorites are personal — other guests don't see which photos you've added.

## Deleting photos

Hosts can delete photos from the event album. Deleted photos move to the **Deleted** album and can be recovered for a limited period before they're permanently removed.

## Per-event albums

Every event automatically gets its own album. When the event ends, the album stays accessible so you and your guests can revisit the memories at any time.
