Every event gets one album. **Only the host can add photos.** Guests can view them, mark favorites, and come back to them after the event. Open **Photos** from the Event Page and click **Upload** to add yours.

<Note>
  Guests can't upload photos to the event album. If guests want to share their pictures, ask them to send them to you and add the ones you'd like to keep.
</Note>

## Photo albums

Photos are organized into albums. Browse by album type:

| Album type | What's here |
|------------|-------------|
| **My Albums** | Albums you've created or uploaded to |
| **Host Albums** | Albums created by the event host |
| **Favorites** | Photos you've marked as a favorite |
| **Deleted** | Recently deleted photos (trash) |

## Viewing photos

Open your event and go to the **Photos** tab to see all photos for that event. You can filter by album type.

## Adding photos

Only the host adds photos. Tap **Add Photos** and select images from your device. They're added to the event album, and everyone invited can see them.

## Adding photos to Favorites

Tap the heart icon on any photo to add it to your **Favorites** album. Favorites are personal — other guests don't see which photos you've added.

## Deleting photos

The host can delete photos from the event album. Deleted photos move to the **Deleted** album and can be recovered for a limited period before they're permanently removed.

## Per-event albums

Every event automatically gets its own album. When the event ends, the album stays open so your guests can come back to the pictures at any time.
