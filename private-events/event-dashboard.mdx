The Event Dashboard is a host and co-host only view that gives you a real-time overview of your event — guest activity, RSVPs, tasks, and more — all from a single screen.

## Access

The Event Dashboard is available to **hosts and co-hosts only**. Guests cannot access it.

<Note>
  The Event Dashboard is available on desktop and tablet browsers only. It is not accessible on mobile.
</Note>

## Opening the dashboard

From the web app, open your event and navigate to the **Dashboard** view. You'll see your event's command center with live metrics and activity at a glance.

## What's on the dashboard

The dashboard gives you a high-level view of your event's health — how many guests have RSVPd, what tasks are outstanding, what's been posted in the activity feed, and other key signals as the event date approaches.

Use it as your go-to screen during the final days of planning and on the day itself to monitor what's happening without switching between tabs.

<Tip>
  Keep the Event Dashboard open on a tablet or laptop during your event so you can see arrivals, activity feed posts, and other real-time updates at a glance.
</Tip>
