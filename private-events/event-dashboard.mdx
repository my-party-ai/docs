You set an event up on the Event Page. You run it from the **Event Dashboard**. The dashboard is a host and co-host only view that gives you a real-time overview of your event — RSVPs or registrations, check-ins, tasks and what guests have shared through your event tools — all from a single screen.

## Access

The Event Dashboard is available to **hosts and co-hosts only**. Guests cannot access it.

<Note>
  The Event Dashboard is available on desktop and tablet browsers only. It is not accessible on mobile.
</Note>

## Opening the dashboard

From the web app, open your event and navigate to the **Dashboard** view. You'll see your event's command center with live metrics and activity at a glance.

## What's on the dashboard

The dashboard gives you a high-level view of your event's health — how many guests have RSVP'd, what tasks are outstanding, what your guests have told you through your event tools, and other key signals as the event date approaches.

Use it as your go-to screen during the final days of planning and on the day itself to monitor what's happening without switching between tabs.

## Event broadcast

Message all your guests at once, right from the dashboard: a change of plan, a reminder of what to bring, or a thank-you afterward. Guests are notified on their own channels (WhatsApp, email, push and in-app). [How broadcasts work →](/organizations/broadcasts#event-broadcasts)

<Note>
  For news you want guests to find again later, post it on the [event wall](/notifications/event-wall) instead. It stays on the Event Page.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Keep the Event Dashboard open on a tablet or laptop during your event so you can see arrivals, check-ins and other real-time updates at a glance.
</Tip>
