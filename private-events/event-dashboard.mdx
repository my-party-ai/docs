You set an event up on the Event Page. You run it from the **Event Dashboard**. Click **Dashboard** on the Event Page to open it.

<Note>
  The Event Dashboard is for **hosts and co-hosts only**, on a laptop or tablet browser. Guests never see it.
</Note>

## The left column: how the event is doing

| Panel | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Event Health Score** | A score out of 100 for how ready the event is, with the days to go. Below it: revenue, check-ins and how much of your task list is done. |
| **RSVPs** or **Registrations** | Your headcount. For a Registration Event, registrations against capacity, broken down by tier. |
| **Guest Preference** | What guests have chosen to eat and drink so far, next to projections for the full guest list. |
| **Revenue** | For paid events, estimated and collected. |
| **Check-In** | How many have arrived. |
| **Tasks** | Created, in progress and completed. |

Click the expand icon on any panel for the full view.

## Multi-day events

For an event that runs over several days, filter the whole dashboard by **All Days** or a single day, such as **Day 1**.

## The tabs

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Timeline** | Every activity from your [Event Schedule](/private-events/event-schedule) in order, with what's up next and how many are checked in to each. |
| **Attendees** / **Guests** | Everyone on the event and their status. |
| **Alerts** | Things that need your attention, sorted into **Critical**, **Informational** and **Recommendation**. |
| **Travel & Stay** | Guests' arrivals and rooms, for planning pickups. [Travel & Stay →](/private-events/travel-stay) |
| **Mini Apps** | Every [event tool](/events/event-tools) with its status at a glance: activities scheduled, preferences saved, contributions received, tasks created. |
| **Custom Message** | Send an event broadcast to your guests. |

## Event broadcast

On the **Custom Message** tab, click **Send Broadcast** to message all your guests at once: a change of plan, a reminder of what to bring, or a thank-you afterward.

<Frame>
  <img

Add a subject and your message, attach an image if you like, and click **Continue to preview**. Guests are notified on their own channels: WhatsApp, email, push and in-app. [How broadcasts work →](/organizations/broadcasts#event-broadcasts)

For news you want guests to find again later, post it on the [event wall](/notifications/event-wall) instead. It stays on the Event Page.

<Tip>
  Keep the dashboard open on a tablet or laptop during the event, so you can see arrivals, check-ins and alerts as they happen.
</Tip>
