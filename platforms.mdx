Fast Party works on the web, on your phone, and inside the AI assistant you already use. It's one account everywhere, so an event you create on your phone is instantly on the web and in Claude.

## Web

Go to [app.fastparty.ai](https://app.fastparty.ai) in any modern browser. There's nothing to install, and every feature is available.

## Guests don't need the app

Guests and attendees receive a link by WhatsApp, SMS or email. They can RSVP, register, pay and check in from that link in any browser. To open the full event page, they sign up for a free Fast Party account.

## Claude and ChatGPT

Fast Party also works inside Claude and ChatGPT. You add it once as a **custom connector** using our server address, then manage your events by conversation. It isn't listed in a connector store, so follow the setup steps.

## Everything stays in sync

RSVPs, registrations, potluck sign-ups and task changes appear everywhere in real time, whether they were made in the app, on the web or through your AI.
