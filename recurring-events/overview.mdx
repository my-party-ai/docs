A circle is a group of people you plan with again and again: your family, the Sunday football lot, a book club, an office team or a college batch. **Add them once.** Every event you run inside the circle already knows who's invited, and posts, polls and surveys keep everyone connected between events.

## Personal and organization circles

|  | Personal circle | Organization circle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who creates it** | You, for your own groups | An organization's admins and managers |
| **Cost** | Free | Part of an organization's paid plan |
| **Events** | RSVP and Registration Events | RSVP, Registration and [Open Events](/events/open-events) |
| **Messages come from** | Fast Party | The organization's own WhatsApp and email |
| **Members** | Invited to the circle | Join the organization once, then placed in any of its circles |

This page covers what every circle does. For circles inside an organization, see [Circles and events in your organization](/organizations/circles-and-events).

## Inside a circle

| Area | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Banner and name** | The circle's banner, photo, name and member count. **Edit banner** changes the image. |
| **About** | What the circle is for, and who manages it. |
| **Events** | The circle's upcoming events. **Manage Events** opens the full list, where you create new ones. |
| **Members** | Everyone in the circle and their role, with **Add Members** at the top. |
| **Posts & Polls** | The circle's feed. **Create** starts a post, poll or survey. |

Switch between **Members** and **Posts & Polls** with the buttons on the right edge of the page.

The **⋮** menu next to the circle's name has **Edit Circle**, **Settings**, **Delete Circle** and **Exit Circle**.

## Members

### How people join

When you add someone, they get an invitation on WhatsApp or email. The member list has three views:

| View | Who's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Members** | People who have joined. |
| **Pending** | People invited who haven't responded yet. |
| **Denied** | People who declined. |

Members don't need the app. They can accept and take part from the link on the web.

### Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Organizer** | Runs the circle: adds and removes members, creates events, posts, and edits the circle. |
| **Member** | Sees the circle's events and feed, RSVPs or registers, and takes part in posts, polls and surveys. |

Open the **⋮** menu next to a member to **Make Organizer** or **Remove Member**. Removing someone doesn't affect events they've already been invited to.

Members can leave a circle at any time with **Exit Circle**.

## Events in a circle

From the circle page, click **Manage Events**. Here you see every event in the circle and create new ones.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/circle-manage-events-1.png"
    alt="Circle Manage Events 1"
    style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }}
    title="Circle Manage Events 1"
  />
</Frame>

- **Filter** by **All**, **Live**, **Not Live** or **Past**.
- **Switch view** between **Tile** and **Calendar**.
- **Set Reminder** on an event to nudge members.
- **Create Event** to add a new one, linked to the circle.

| Type | In a circle |
| --- | --- |
| [**RSVP Event**](/private-events/event-creation) | Every member is invited and RSVPs. |
| [**Registration Event**](/public-events/overview) | Members sign up through the event's link, with or without your approval, and pay if the event is priced. |
| [**Open Event**](/events/open-events) | Every member is welcome, with no RSVP needed. Organization circles only. |

Like every event, a circle event can run over several days, with a different invitation template for each day.

### Not live, then live

A circle event starts **Not Live**. Members can see it's coming, but can't open it yet, so you have time to set it up. When you make it **Live**, every member is invited at once, on their own channels.

<Warning>
  Making an event live invites the whole circle, and a notification can't be taken back. Check the venue, time, description and invitation template first.
</Warning>

### Adding a circle to another event

When adding guests to any RSVP Event, click **Add from Circle** and choose a circle. All its members are added in one step. To leave someone out, remove them from that event's guest list; the circle itself doesn't change.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is a circle free?">
    Yes. Personal circles are free to create, and inviting members costs nothing. Organizations, with many circles under one roster and brand, are part of our paid plans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does everyone in my circle need the app?">
    No. Members can join, RSVP, register and use every event tool from a link on the web. The app is there for those who want it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can members see who else is in the circle?">
    Yes, unless you turn on **Hide Member List** when you create the circle.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I run a paid event in a circle?">
    Yes. Create a Registration Event in the circle and set a price. Payments go straight to your own account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I manage a small group. Is a circle still useful?">
    If you plan anything together more than once, yes. The second event takes minutes because everyone is already there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
