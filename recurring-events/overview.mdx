A circle is a saved group of people: your family, a work team, a book club, a college batch or a club section. Add members once, and you can run event after event with the same group without re-entering names and contacts.

</Frame>

Circles can be **personal**, created by you for your own groups, or part of an **organization**, where admins and managers run them. [Circles in an organization →](/organizations/circles-and-events)

## Create a circle

Go to **Circles** and click **New Circle**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name your circle">
    Give it a clear name your members will recognize, up to 30 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a cover image">
    Upload a photo or banner so the circle is easy to spot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a description">
    Optional. Describe what the group is for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Enter each member's name and a phone number or email. They receive an invitation to join. You can also share the circle's invite link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Your circle is ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can add or remove members at any time. Changes to a circle don't affect guests already added to past events.

## What's in a circle

| Tab | What's here |
|-----|-------------|
| **About** | The circle's description, member count and settings |
| **Events** | Events created in or linked to this circle |
| **Members** | The full member list, with management options |
| **Posts & Polls** | The circle's feed: posts, polls and surveys |

## Events in a circle

From a circle's **Events** tab, create an event that's automatically linked to the circle. Any of the three event types works:

| Type | In a circle |
| --- | --- |
| **RSVP Event** | Invite circle members, or anyone else, and track who's coming. Add all members to the guest list in one click. |
| **Registered Event** | Members sign up through a link, with or without your approval, and pay if the event is priced. |
| [**Open Event**](/events/open-events) | Every member of the circle is welcome, with no RSVP needed. Open Events can only be created inside a circle, and are shared only with its members. |

This is how a one-off becomes a series: a monthly book club, a weekly club night or an annual reunion, all with the same group.

### Adding a circle to another event

When adding guests to any RSVP Event, click **Add from Circle** and choose a circle. All its members are added at once. To leave someone out, remove them from that event's guest list. The circle itself doesn't change.

## Posts, polls and surveys

Every circle has a feed that keeps members connected between events.

- **Posts:** share updates, photos and news. Members can react.
- **Polls:** ask a quick question, such as which date works best, and let members vote.
- **Surveys:** ask several questions at once. Describe what you want to find out, and a draft survey is written for you. Review and edit the questions, then publish it to the circle.

## Find and join circles

Browse and search public circles from the **Circles** page. If a circle is open, you can join it directly.

## Manage your circles

From a circle's settings you can:

- Edit the member list
- Rename the circle
- Update the cover image
- Delete the circle. Events the circle has already been used for aren't affected.
