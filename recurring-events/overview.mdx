A circle is a group of people you plan with again and again: your family, the Sunday football lot, a book club, an office team or a college batch. **Add them once.** Every event you run inside the circle already knows who's invited, and posts and polls keep everyone connected between events.

## Personal and organization circles

|  | Personal circle | Organization circle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who creates it** | You, for your own groups | An organization's admins and managers |
| **Cost** | Free | Part of an organization's paid plan |
| **Events** | RSVP and Registration Events | RSVP, Registration and [Open Events](/events/open-events) |
| **Messages come from** | Fast Party | The organization's own WhatsApp and email |
| **Members** | Invited to the circle | Join the organization once, then placed in any of its circles |

This page covers what every circle does. For how circles work inside an organization, see [Circles and events in your organization](/organizations/circles-and-events).

## Create a circle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new circle">
    From **Home**, go to **My Circles** and click **\+**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and describe it">
    Give it a name your members will recognize, and a short line on what the group is for. The description shows on the circle's **About** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a banner">
    A wide image across the top of the circle makes it easy to spot. Upload your own, or generate one with AI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Click **Add Members** and enter each person's phone number or email. They're sent an invitation on WhatsApp or email to join.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Connected to Claude or ChatGPT? Say "Create a circle called Weekend Riders with these 20 people and make a banner for it," and it's done. [Connect your AI →](/platforms#connect-your-ai)
</Tip>

## Inside a circle

Every circle page has the same layout.

| Area | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Banner and name** | The circle's banner, name and member count. Click **Edit banner** to change the image. |
| **About** | What the circle is for, and who manages it. |
| **Events** | The circle's upcoming events. **Manage Events** takes you to the circle's event list, where you create new ones. |
| **Members** | Everyone in the circle and their role. Switch between **Members**, **Pending** and **Denied** to see who has joined, who hasn't responded, and who declined. |
| **Posts & Polls** | The circle's feed. Click **Create** to share a post, run a poll or send a survey. |

Use the **Members** and **Posts & Polls** buttons on the right edge of the page to switch between the member list and the feed.

## Members

### How people join

When you add someone, they get an invitation on WhatsApp or email. Until they accept, they're listed under **Pending**. If they decline, they move to **Denied**. Members don't need the app: they can accept and take part from the link on the web.

### Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Organizer** | Runs the circle: adds and removes members, creates events, posts, and edits the circle. |
| **Member** | Sees the circle's events and feed, RSVPs or registers, reacts to posts and votes in polls. |

To remove someone, open the menu next to their name. Removing a member doesn't affect events they've already been invited to.

### Leaving and muting

Members can **mute** a circle to stop its messages while staying in it, or **leave** it at any time.

## Events in a circle

From the circle's **Events** tab, click **Manage Events** and then **Create Event**. The event is linked to the circle.

| Type | In a circle |
| --- | --- |
| [**RSVP Event**](/private-events/event-creation) | Every member is invited and RSVPs. |
| [**Registration Event**](/public-events/overview) | Members sign up through the event's link, with or without your approval, and pay if the event is priced. |
| [**Open Event**](/events/open-events) | Every member is welcome, with no RSVP needed. Organization circles only. |

Like every event, a circle event can run over several days, with a different invitation template for each day.

### Created, then live

A circle event has two states:

| State | What members see |
| --- | --- |
| **Created** | The event is listed as upcoming, but members can't open it yet. This is your time to set it up. |
| **Live** | Every member is invited at once, on their own channels. |

<Warning>
  Making an event live invites the whole circle, and a notification can't be taken back. Check the venue, time, description and invitation template first.
</Warning>

### Adding a circle to another event

When adding guests to any RSVP Event, click **Add from Circle** and choose a circle. All its members are added in one step. To leave someone out, remove them from that event's guest list; the circle itself doesn't change.

## Posts, polls and surveys

The feed keeps the group together between events. Click **Create** in **Posts & Polls** and choose:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Post" icon="message">
    Share an update, news or photos. Members react to posts.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Poll" icon="square-poll-vertical">
    Ask one question with a few options, such as which date works best. Set how long it stays open; you can close it early or extend it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Survey" icon="clipboard-question">
    Ask several questions at once. Describe what you want to find out, and a draft is written for you to review, edit and publish.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  A poll is the quickest way to get a quiet group talking. "Which Saturday works for the next meet?" gets more replies than a post.
</Tip>

## Find and join circles

Use **Find a circle** on the Circles page to search the circles you belong to and public circles you can join.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is a circle free?">
    Yes. Personal circles are free to create, and inviting members costs nothing. Organizations, with many circles under one roster and brand, are part of our paid plans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does everyone in my circle need the app?">
    No. Members can join, RSVP, register and use every event tool from a link on the web. The app is there for those who want it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I run a paid event in a circle?">
    Yes. Create a Registration Event in the circle and set a price. Payments go straight to your own account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I manage a small group. Is a circle still useful?">
    If you plan anything together more than once, yes. The second event takes minutes because everyone is already there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
