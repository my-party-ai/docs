Fast Party connects to ChatGPT as a **custom MCP app**. You add it once using the Fast Party MCP server URL, sign in with your Fast Party account, and then manage events directly from ChatGPT.

<Note>
  Custom MCP apps in ChatGPT require developer mode. The connection is per user — each person connects their own Fast Party account.
</Note>

## Steps to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open ChatGPT settings">
    Go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) and sign in. Click your profile name in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar, then select **Settings**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/chatgpt-connect/image1.png" alt="ChatGPT sidebar — open profile menu and click Settings" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to Apps">
    In the Settings modal, select **Apps** from the left menu.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/chatgpt-connect/image2.png" alt="ChatGPT Settings — Apps section in the sidebar" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the Fast Party app">
    Under **Enabled apps**, find **Advanced settings** and click **Create app**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/chatgpt-connect/image3.png" alt="App Preferences — click Create app under Advanced settings" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>

    In the **New App** dialog, enter:
    - **Name:** `FastParty`
    - **Connection type:** Server URL
    - **URL:** `https://chat.fastparty.ai/mcp`
    - **Authentication:** OAuth

    Check **I understand and want to continue**, then click **Create**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/chatgpt-connect/image4.png" alt="New App dialog with FastParty name, MCP URL, and OAuth authentication" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Fast Party appears in your apps list. Open it and click **Connect**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/chatgpt-connect/image5.png" alt="FastParty app details — click Connect" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in with Fast Party">
    An **Add FastParty to ChatGPT** window opens. Click **Sign in with FastParty** to authorise the connection.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/chatgpt-connect/image6.png" alt="Add FastParty to ChatGPT — click Sign in with FastParty" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in to your Fast Party account">
    Sign in with your Fast Party account — using Google, Microsoft, phone number, or email. If you don't have an account yet, you can sign up at [app.fastparty.ai/signup](https://app.fastparty.ai/signup).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/chatgpt-connect/image7.png" alt="Sign in to Fast Party with phone number or email" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your phone">
    If you sign in with a phone number, enter the 6-digit code sent to your phone, then click **Continue**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/chatgpt-connect/image8.png" alt="Check your phone — enter the verification code" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="You're connected">
    Fast Party is now connected to ChatGPT. In **Settings → Apps**, you can see the connection details and manage permissions. Click **Refresh** if the connection status doesn't update right away.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/chatgpt-connect/image9.png" alt="FastParty connected — app preferences and connection details" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use Fast Party in a chat">
    Start a new chat. Click the **+** icon in the message bar, select **More**, then choose **FastParty**. Fast Party appears in the composer and is ready to use.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/chatgpt-connect/image10.png" alt="Message bar — click + then More and select FastParty" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>

    With Fast Party selected, ask ChatGPT to manage your events — for example, "What events do I have coming up?" or "Create a dinner party for next Friday."

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/chatgpt-connect/image11.png" alt="FastParty selected in the ChatGPT message composer" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What permissions are requested?

When you connect Fast Party to ChatGPT, you're giving ChatGPT the ability to:

- Create and update your events
- Add guests and send invitations
- View RSVP statuses
- Set up agendas, tasks, food preferences, and potlucks
- Create and manage guest circles

Fast Party only accesses your data when you ask ChatGPT to do something. It doesn't run in the background or make changes without your instruction.

## What you can do via ChatGPT

The same Fast Party capabilities available through Claude are available through ChatGPT:

- Create and edit events
- Invite guests and track RSVPs
- Build and publish event schedules
- Set up food & beverage preferences
- Create and manage potluck sign-ups
- Create and assign tasks
- Manage registered events and approve registrants
- Set up and manage Circles

## Example prompts

> "Create a dinner party for next Friday at 7 PM at my place, for 12 guests."

> "Add a potluck to my birthday party — mains, sides, desserts, 3 slots each — and publish it."

> "Show me the RSVPs for my housewarming event."

> "Set up a conference agenda: registration at 9 AM, keynote at 10, lunch at 12:30, workshops at 2 PM."

> "Approve all pending registrations for my charity gala."

## Disconnecting

To remove the connection, go to **Settings → Apps**, open Fast Party, and disconnect the app.

Disconnecting removes ChatGPT's access to your Fast Party account. Your Fast Party data is never affected — everything stays intact in Fast Party.
