## Setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open ChatGPT settings">
    Go to your ChatGPT account settings and navigate to the integrations or connectors section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Fast Party connector">
    Search for Fast Party in the connector marketplace or integrations list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your account">
    Follow the prompts to authorise Fast Party to connect to your ChatGPT account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start managing events through conversation">
    Once connected, ask ChatGPT to create events, invite guests, set up agendas, and more.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do via ChatGPT

The same Fast Party capabilities available through Claude are available through ChatGPT:

- Create and edit events
- Invite guests and track RSVPs
- Build and publish event schedules
- Set up food & beverage preferences
- Create and manage potluck sign-ups
- Create and assign tasks
- Manage registered events and approve registrants
- Set up and manage Circles

## Example prompts

> "Create a dinner party for next Friday at 7 PM at my place, for 12 guests."

> "Add a potluck to my birthday party — mains, sides, desserts, 3 slots each — and publish it."

> "Show me the RSVPs for my housewarming event."

> "Set up a conference agenda: registration at 9 AM, keynote at 10, lunch at 12:30, workshops at 2 PM."

> "Approve all pending registrations for my charity gala."
