Fast Party is an intelligent event planning and management platform for everything from an intimate dinner or birthday to a 500-person conference. Whatever you're planning, it comes down to two things: which **event type** you choose, and which features you switch on.

Whether you're a host putting together a private gathering or an organizer running meetings, conferences, and exhibitions, the same platform handles the details — guests, invitations, schedules, food preferences, tasks, vendors, and more — all in one place.

Fast Party works on the web at https://app.fastparty.ai, or download the app on iOS or Android mobile platforms.

Android App link: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.fastparty.mobile

iOS App link: https://apps.apple.com/in/app/fast-party/id6748051479

**Plan online. Live offline.**

**Meet Antsy, working behind the scenes**

Antsy is Fast Party's built-in intelligence layer. It works quietly in the background — smart nudges, reminders, and predictions that keep your event on track, so nothing slips through the cracks as the date approaches.

**Or plan through conversation, with Claude or ChatGPT**

Fast Party connects to both Claude and ChatGPT through our MCP server. Once configured, you can manage your entire account through natural conversation — create events, invite guests, build agendas, assign tasks, and more, without opening the app. Just tell the AI what you need.


The big picture: 

Two event types, one optional layer
Almost everything in Fast Party builds on three ideas. Understand these and the rest of the docs will make sense quickly.
<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="Regular Event" icon="calendar">
    An invitation-based event. You control the guest list, and invited guests RSVP directly. No registration queue, no approvals. Best for birthdays, dinners, weddings, family gatherings, meetings, and private functions.
</Card>
<Card title="Registered Event" icon="ticket">
    A registration-based event. You open a registration link, and people request a spot — you approve or decline each one. Supports ticket types, capacity limits, and payments. Best for conferences, exhibitions, ticketed gatherings, and any event where you vet attendees.
</Card>
</CardGroup>
And the layer that sits on top of both:
<Card title="My Circles — make any event recurring" icon="rotate">
  A **Circle** is a saved group of people. Add your members once, then run event after event with the same group without re-entering names. This is how a one-off event becomes a **recurring** one — a monthly book club, a quarterly townhall, or a conference that returns every year. Either event type can live inside a Circle.
</Card>
### Which one do I need?
| If you're running… | Use | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A birthday, dinner, wedding, meeting, or private function | **Regular Event** | You know exactly who's invited and want direct RSVPs. |
| A conference, exhibition, or ticketed event | **Registered Event** | You need a registration link, approvals, capacity, and (optionally) ticket sales. |
| A series with the same group (book club, townhalls, an annual conference) | **A Circle** containing Regular *or* Registered events | Add members once; reuse them across every event in the series. |
