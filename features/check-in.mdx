Fast Party gives you multiple ways to track arrivals at your event. You can check guests in manually, let guests self-check-in, or use QR codes at the door.

## Host manual check-in

As the host (or co-host), you can check in guests one by one from the **Guest Check-In** page:

1. Open your event and go to **Guest Check-In**.
2. Find the guest in the list — search by name or scroll.
3. Tap the check-in button next to their name to mark them as arrived.

The guest list updates in real time, so you can see at a glance who has arrived and who hasn't.

## Guest self-check-in

Guests can check themselves in when they arrive. From their invitation link, they tap **Check In** to confirm they've arrived. The host's check-in view updates automatically.

## QR code check-in

For faster check-in at the door, you can use QR codes:

- A QR code is available from the **Agenda** page (in the Guest Check-In modal).
- Display the QR code at the entrance — on a screen, a printed sign, or a tablet.
- Guests scan the code with their phone camera to check themselves in instantly.

<Tip>
  QR code check-in works well for events with a steady stream of arrivals — you don't need a person at the door manually checking off names.
</Tip>

## Check-in with Registered Events

For **Registered Events**, check-in integrates with the registration system:

- Only guests who have been approved and have a valid registration order can be checked in.
- The check-in view shows registration details alongside each guest's name.
- You can verify ticket types and registration info at the door.

<Note>
  See [Registered Events](/features/registered-events) for more on setting up registration, ticket types, and the registration dashboard.
</Note>

## Check-in from the agenda

The Guest Check-In view is also accessible from the **Agenda** page. This is useful if you're managing the agenda and arrivals from the same screen during your event.
