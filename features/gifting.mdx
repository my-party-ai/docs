Fast Party's gifting features let guests send digital gifts and heartfelt wishes directly through the event — no separate app or payment link needed.

## Enabling gifts

Gifts are off by default. To turn them on, open your event, go to **Gifting**, and toggle gifts on. Once enabled, guests will see the gifting options from their invitation view.

## Digital Gifting

Digital Gifting lets guests send cash gifts electronically. Three methods are supported:

**UPI QR code**
Upload your UPI QR code so guests can scan it and pay directly through their preferred UPI app.

**Payment screenshot**
Guests can complete a transfer via any method and upload a screenshot as proof of payment. You'll see the screenshots in the gifting dashboard.

**Gift cards**
Guests can send digital gift cards, which are collected and displayed in your gifting summary.

All received digital gifts appear in the **Gifting Summary** view, giving you a quick overview of what you've received.

## Best Wishes

Best Wishes lets guests send a personal message — a note, a memory, or a warm wish — attached to your event.

**For guests:**
- Write a message up to **2,000 characters**.
- Optionally attach up to **2 media files** (photos or videos).
- Submit the wish from their invitation link.

**For hosts:**
- Customise the **banner text** shown on the Best Wishes page — set a personal prompt or a themed heading.
- View all submitted wishes in one place from the event dashboard.

<Tip>
  Best Wishes is a lovely alternative to a guestbook at events like weddings, milestone birthdays, and retirement parties. Guests can submit their wishes before, during, or after the event.
</Tip>

## Disabling gifts

You can toggle gifting off at any time from the **Gifting** settings. If you disable it after guests have already submitted wishes or gifts, the existing submissions are retained for you to view, but new submissions are no longer accepted.
