Fast Party makes it easy to invite guests, send them everything they need, and track their responses in real time.

## Adding guests

Guests can be invited to RSVP to the event via phone number or by email.

### Via Mobile App

- **Share invite link** — share the event invite via WhatsApp, email, or any other channel directly from the app.
- **Add guest** — enter a guest's phone number or email address. You can also import contacts from your phone book, Google, or Microsoft Outlook and invite them directly.

### Via Web

All mobile options are available on web, plus one additional option:

- **CSV upload** — upload a spreadsheet with columns: FirstName, LastName, Email, Phone. A CSV template is available to download from the guests page.

## Sending invitations

When you add a guest, Fast Party sends them an invitation email automatically. The email includes the event name, date, time, location, and a personal RSVP link.

<Note>
  Guests do not need a Fast Party account to RSVP. They click the link in the email and respond directly.
</Note>

If you'd prefer to add guests without sending invitations immediately, you can hold invitations and send them manually when you're ready.

## RSVP tracking

Each guest has one of four RSVP statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Invitation sent, no response yet |
| **Accepted** | Guest is coming |
| **Declined** | Guest can't make it |
| **Maybe** | Guest is unsure |

Your event dashboard shows a live RSVP count. You can filter the guest list by status and send a reminder to anyone who hasn't responded yet.

## Registered Events and registration approval

For **Registered Events**, the RSVP flow is different. Guests don't receive a direct invitation — instead, they submit a registration request which you must approve before they're confirmed.

- Pending registrations appear in the **Registrants** tab of your event dashboard.
- You can approve or decline individual registrations, or bulk-approve all pending requests at once.
- Approved guests receive a confirmation and are added to the guest list.
- Declined guests are notified that their request was not accepted.

<Tip>
  Registered Events are useful for ticketed events, events with limited capacity, or any gathering where you want to vet attendees. See [Registered Events](/features/registered-events) for the full setup guide including ticket types, pricing, and the registration dashboard.
</Tip>

## Guest details

For each guest you can see:

- Name and email address
- RSVP status
- Dietary preference (if food preferences are enabled)
- Potluck contribution (if potluck is enabled)

## Removing guests

You can remove a guest from an event at any time. If they've already received an invitation, they'll get an email letting them know they've been removed.

<Tip>
  Use Circles to save groups of guests you invite regularly — family, work team, book club — so you can add everyone in one click for future events.
</Tip>
