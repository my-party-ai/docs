Arrivals & Stays lets guests share their travel logistics — arrival time, accommodation details, and departure — so you can coordinate pickups, room assignments, and departures for multi-day events.

<Note>
  Arrivals & Stays is most useful for destination events, retreats, house parties, and weddings where guests are travelling from out of town or staying overnight.
</Note>

## How guests submit their details

Guests open the **Arrivals & Stays** section from their invitation link and complete a 4-step wizard:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Arrival">
    - Arrival date and time
    - Luggage count: **No Bags**, **1–2 bags**, **3–4 bags**, or **5+ bags**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Room">
    - Bed configuration preference: **King Bed**, **Twin Beds**, or **Double + Single**
    - Check-in date and time
    - Check-out date and time
  </Step>
  <Step title="Departure">
    - Departure date and time
    - Luggage count (with a souvenir variant for guests who pick up gifts or items during the event)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Guests review their submitted details and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Host management view

As the host, you see all submitted arrival and stay details in one place. This gives you a consolidated view of:

- Who is arriving and when
- Luggage counts (useful for coordinating transport or porters)
- Room preferences and check-in/check-out windows
- Departure times

Use this information to plan pickups, assign rooms, arrange check-in staff, and coordinate departures without chasing individual guests.

<Tip>
  Share the Arrivals & Stays link with guests a few weeks before the event so you have time to act on the information — arrange transport, confirm room assignments, and brief any staff.
</Tip>
