Antsy is Fast Party's intelligence layer. It's how Fast Party talks with you inside the app: pointing out what needs your attention, answering your questions, and guiding you through each step.

You'll find it behind the **Ask Antsy** button, in the same place on every screen.

## Available today

Tap **Ask Antsy** from any screen to open the Ask Antsy page:

* **Nudges across all your events.** Guests who haven't RSVP'd, tasks coming due, sections still unpublished: everything that needs attention, in one place.
* **Help and how-to videos.** Short guides on using Fast Party, right where you need them.

## Coming soon: Ask Antsy chat

<Note>
  **Coming soon.** Ask Antsy chat is rolling out in phases. Phase 1 is described below.
</Note>

Ask Antsy becomes a chat on every page. Tap the button and a panel opens beside the page (a sheet from the bottom on mobile). Ask anything about what you're doing, or let Antsy walk you through it one question at a time.

### Phase 1: help getting started

Phase 1 is for the moments when you're new and not sure where to begin. Antsy guides you on five pages:

| Page | How Antsy helps |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Greets you and offers a few starting points: create an event, create a circle, or go to your organization. |
| **Create Event** | Asks what kind of event you're planning, then fills in the details with you as you answer. |
| **Registration setup** | Walks you through tickets, capacity and approval for a Registered Event. |
| **Create Circle** | Helps you name and set up a new circle. |
| **Create Organization** | Explains what an organization includes and takes the details our team needs to get you set up. |

### How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask, or answer">
    Type a question at any time, or answer Antsy's questions. Most answers are a tap on a suggestion, so you rarely need to type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Antsy fills in the page">
    As you answer, Antsy fills in the fields on the page. You can change anything it fills in, and your changes always win.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You press the button">
    When everything's ready, Antsy hands the last step back to you. It never presses **Create**, **Publish**, **Send** or **Invite** for you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What to expect

- **It opens by itself only when you're new.** For example, on your first visit to Home or to Create Event. It won't pop up again after you close it, and it never interrupts you while you're working.
- **It stays on the page you're on.** Antsy helps with the task in front of you rather than sending you elsewhere.
- **Bigger jobs go to your own AI.** For things like planning a whole schedule, working with large files or writing in bulk, Antsy suggests [connecting Claude or ChatGPT](/platforms#connect-your-ai), which can do the work across your whole workspace.

### Later phases

Next, Ask Antsy chat comes to the Event Page, guest lists and invitations, and each event tool, and then to the Event Dashboard and your organization.

## Antsy and your own AI

|  | Antsy | Claude or ChatGPT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Where** | Inside Fast Party, on every screen | In your own AI assistant, outside the app |
| **Best for** | Quick answers, and guidance through the page you're on | Doing larger jobs across your whole workspace by conversation |
| **Setup** | None. It's built in | Connect once. [Connect your AI →](/platforms#connect-your-ai) |

They work together: Antsy helps you in the moment, and your own AI takes on the bigger work.
