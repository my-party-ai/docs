Say hello to **Antsy**, the friendly ant behind Fast Party's intelligence layer. Antsy is here to guide you through the platform, so you always know what a page is for and what to do next.

## Why an ant?

We chose an ant for two reasons.

**Its character.** Ants are hardworking, relentless, organized and brilliant at working together, which is exactly what it takes to pull off a great event. That's Antsy: always busy in the background, and always ready to lend a hand.

**Everyone knows an ant.** Ants are universal, so Antsy can star in any story: ants getting married, ants at a conference, ants throwing a birthday party or running a club night. Whatever you're planning, there's an Antsy for it.

## Ask Antsy

<Note>
  **Coming at the end of September.** Ask Antsy will be available on every page of Fast Party.
</Note>

Whenever you need help, **Ask Antsy**. Look for the Ask Antsy button, in the same place on every page. Tap it and Ask Antsy opens right beside the page you're on (from the bottom of the screen on mobile).

### It guides you every step of the way

Ask Antsy walks you through what you're doing, one step at a time:

- **Suggestion chips** you tap to answer or choose what to do next, so you rarely need to type
- **Hints** that explain what each part of the page is for and what to do first

### Ask it anything

You can also type to Ask Antsy at any time:

- **Questions about the page you're on:** "What's the difference between an RSVP Event and a Registered Event?"
- **Help with anything in Fast Party:** "How do I add a co-host?"
- **Getting around:** "Take me to my circles." Ask Antsy takes you straight to the page you need.

## Ask Antsy and your own AI

Ask Antsy guides you inside Fast Party. For bigger jobs across your whole workspace, connect your own AI.

|  | Ask Antsy | Claude or ChatGPT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Where** | Inside Fast Party, on every page | In your own AI assistant |
| **Best for** | Step-by-step guidance, questions about the page, and getting around | Larger jobs across your workspace, such as building a full schedule or inviting a whole circle |
| **Setup** | None. It's built in | Connect once. [Connect your AI →](/platforms#connect-your-ai) |
