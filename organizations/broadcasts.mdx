A broadcast is a direct message to a group of people: an announcement, a reminder or an appeal. There are two kinds.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Event broadcast" icon="calendar">
    From the **Event Dashboard**, to the guests of one event.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Organization broadcast" icon="building">
    From your organization's **Admin Dashboard**, to the members of any of its circles.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Event broadcasts

Hosts and co-hosts can message everyone on an event from the [Event Dashboard](/private-events/event-dashboard).

- Use it for news that belongs with this event: a change of plan, a reminder of what to bring, a thank-you afterward.
- Guests are notified on their channels, the same way as an [Update](/notifications/message-types#update).
- For news you want guests to find again later, a post on the [event wall](/notifications/event-wall) keeps it on the Event Page.

## Organization broadcasts

Admins can message any circle. Managers can message the circles they run.

**Each person receives a broadcast only once**, even if they belong to several of the circles you picked.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Broadcast">
    On the Admin Dashboard, open **Broadcast** and tap **Send Broadcast**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the circles">
    In **Send to**, pick one or more circles, or **Select all**. To reach everyone in your organization, pick **All Members**. You see how many members will receive it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Add a subject (optional) and your message. You can attach an image too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    Tap **Continue to preview** to see the message as it will arrive by email and on WhatsApp, with how many people get each. Nothing is sent until you tap **Send broadcast**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every broadcast is also posted to each chosen circle's updates feed, so members who join later still see it.

To message one event's guests instead, tap **Choose an event** at the top of Send Broadcast.

### Pin to the organization page

Admins can tap **Pin to org page** on a sent broadcast to show it under **Announcements** on your [organization page](/organizations/org-page), where every member can find it again.

## Choosing the right channel

| Channel | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp** | News tied to an event, such as a change of venue. |
| **Email** | General announcements, newsletters and longer messages. |

Social updates never go on WhatsApp. Messages go out from your organization's own WhatsApp number and email address, so set those up before you start. See [Your WhatsApp & Email](/notifications/your-channels).

## Broadcast history

Every broadcast you send is listed on the Broadcast page, with who sent it and when.
