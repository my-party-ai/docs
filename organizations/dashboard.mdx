The Admin Dashboard is where you run your organization. Just as an Event Dashboard runs one event, the Admin Dashboard covers every circle and event at once. Admins and managers open it from **My Organizations** on their Home page; members see your [organization page](/organizations/org-page) instead.

## The menu

| Item | What it's for | Admin | Manager |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | What's next and what needs your attention. | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Org Circles** | Every circle in your organization, starting with All Members. [Circles and events →](/organizations/circles-and-events) | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Org Events** | Every event, one-off and circle events, with filters. [Circles and events →](/organizations/circles-and-events#see-every-circle-and-event) | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Org Members** | Your master roster (All Members): add members, add them to circles, remove them. [Org Members →](/organizations/org-members) | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Attention** | Everything waiting on you. [Attention →](/organizations/attention) | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Broadcast** | Message the members of your circles. [Broadcasts →](/organizations/broadcasts) | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Settings** | Six tabs: Organization, Org page, Members and circles, Team, Messaging and Payments. [Set up your organization →](/organizations/setup#settings) | ✓ | Not shown |
| **View as member** | Your organization page the way a member sees it, under a **Member View** banner. [The organization page →](/organizations/org-page) | ✓ | ✓ |

Managers see the same menu without **Settings**, and their lists cover the circles and events they run or are assigned.

The organization switcher in the top bar shows which organization you're in, and your role in each organization you belong to.

## At the top: four tiles

| Tile | What it counts | Tap it to open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Org Circles** | The circles in your organization | Org Circles |
| **Org Events** | Events across your organization | Org Events |
| **Org Members** | People on your roster | Org Members |
| **Needs attention** | Items waiting on you | Attention |

**Create Event** and **Create Circle** sit beside them.

Managers see the same dashboard for the circles and events they run or are assigned, never money or Settings.

## Needs attention

The Dashboard shows what's waiting on you across every circle and one-off event, such as an event that isn't live yet or registrations waiting for approval. Tap the tile, or **Attention** in the menu, for the full list. [Attention →](/organizations/attention)

## Upcoming Org Events and Org Circles

Below that, **Upcoming Org Events** shows what's coming next, and **Org Circles** lists each circle with its members and upcoming events. Tap **View all** on either to open the full list.

<Tip>
  Tap **Ask Antsy** on any page of the Admin Dashboard to ask what something does or where to find it. You can also connect your own AI and ask in plain language: "What needs my attention this week?" [Connect your AI →](/platforms#connect-your-ai)
</Tip>
