Every organization has its own page at `app.fastparty.ai/o/your-link-name`. It's the one place your members see everything from you, and the page people see before they join. Admins decide what goes on it, and who sees each item, in **Settings › Org page**.

## What members see

When a member opens your organization page, they see:

| Part | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Header** | Your logo, name and tagline, how many circles you have (unless you hide member counts), and a **Website** button when you've added a website. |
| **About** | What your organization is about, in your own words. |
| **Contact us** | Your website, contact WhatsApp and email, address, and **Follow us** with your social pages. |
| **My Events** | Their events from your organization, with their reply or registration status. **View all** opens the full list. |
| **My Circles** | The circles they're in. Tap one to open it. |
| **Announcements** | Broadcasts you've pinned to the page. |
| **Links and documents** | Bylaws, forms, brochures and other resources you've shared with members. |
| **Office bearers** | The people who run your organization, if you've shown them. |

At the bottom, **Go to my home** takes them to their own Fast Party Home, where they can plan personal events and start their own circles.

Only items set to **Public** or **Members** appear. Items set to **Hide** never show.

## What visitors see

Someone who isn't a member sees the public view: your header, About, what members get when they join, the names of your circles (not what's inside them) and how to contact you. Only items set to **Public** appear.

## What admins and managers see

Admins and managers run the organization from the [Admin Dashboard](/organizations/dashboard). To see the page the way members do, tap **View as member**: the page opens with a **Member View** banner at the top, so you can check what members see before and after you publish changes.

## Where new members land

When you add someone new to your organization, they join **All Members** and their invitation brings them straight to your organization page. From there they open their events and circles.

Someone who's already a member and is added to another circle goes to that circle's page instead.

## Set up your organization page

Only admins can edit the page. Open the Admin Dashboard, then **Settings › Org page**. Next to most items is a choice of who sees it:

| Setting | Who sees the item |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** | Everyone, including visitors who aren't members |
| **Members** | Only members of your organization |
| **Hide** | Nobody; it stays saved but doesn't show |

Work through the page from top to bottom:

| Item | What to add | Tips |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Banner image** | A wide image across the top of your page. Tap **Add banner** to upload your own or generate one with AI. | Use a wide image, 8:3, about 2731 × 1024 pixels, JPG or PNG. |
| **Tagline** | One line about your organization, up to 160 characters. | For example, the place or the purpose: "Banyan Tree Resort", "Alumni of the Class of 2010". |
| **About** | What your organization is about, up to 2,000 characters. | Line breaks and short lists work well. |
| **Address** | Where people can find you, up to 300 characters. |  |
| **Website** | Your website address. | Adds the **Website** button to your header. |
| **Contact WhatsApp** | A WhatsApp number people can reach you on. | This is a contact number shown on the page. It's not where messages to members are sent from. |
| **Contact email** | An email address people can write to. | Same: shown on the page, not your sending address. |
| **Social pages** | Tap **\+ Add a social page**, pick the platform and paste the link. | Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Strava, WhatsApp channel or Other. They appear under **Follow us**. |
| **Links and documents** | Tap **\+ Add a link or document**. Choose **Link** or **Document**, add the link or file, a title (for example, "Membership form") and an optional description. | Bylaws, forms, brochures and other resources. New items are set to **Members** to start with. |
| **Office bearers** | Tap **\+ Add an office bearer**, choose a person from your roster, and give their title (for example, "President"). | President, secretary, treasurer and others. |

When you're done:

- **Publish** puts your changes on the page for everyone they're set to show to.
- **Save draft** keeps your changes without showing them to anyone yet.
- **Discard changes** throws away what you haven't published.

<Note>
  The contact WhatsApp and email on your page are how visitors reach you. Messages to members go out from your organization's own messaging channels, set up in **Settings › Messaging**. [Your WhatsApp & Email →](/notifications/your-channels)
</Note>

To stop members seeing how many people are in your organization or its circles, or who they are, turn on **Hide member count and member lists** in **Settings › Members and circles**.

## Announcements

A broadcast you pin with **Pin to org page** appears under **Announcements**, so members can find it again later. [Broadcasts →](/organizations/broadcasts)

## For members

- **Your events and circles:** everything from the organization is on this page. Tap an event or a circle to open it.
- **Your details:** other members never see your phone number or email address. The organization's admins can, so they can reach you.
- **Leaving:** you can leave any circle from that circle's page and stay in the organization. To leave the organization itself, contact it through **Contact us**.
- **Your own events:** Fast Party is yours too. **Go to my home** takes you to your own Home.

<Tip>
  Tap **Ask Antsy** on the organization page to ask what's on it, where your events are, or how to leave a circle.
</Tip>
