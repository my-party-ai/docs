Admins run the whole organization. Managers help run the circles and events they're assigned. Everyone else on your roster is a member. You manage your team in **Settings › Team**, which only admins see.

## Settings › Team

The Team tab lists your **Admins** and your **Managers**, each with their role.

| On the tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Admins** | Everyone with full control. Admins are promoted from managers, never invited directly. |
| **Managers** | Everyone who helps run circles and events. Tap **⋮** beside a manager for **Assign manager**, **Make admin** or **Remove**. |
| **Add Manager** | Invite someone new to the team as a manager. |

## Roles at a glance

| | Admin | Manager | Member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See and run **every** circle and event | Yes | No | No |
| Run the circles and events **they create or are assigned** | Yes | Yes | No |
| Create circles and one-off events | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add members to circles | Yes | Into their circles | No |
| Remove someone from the organization | Yes | No | No |
| Send broadcasts | To any circle | To their circles | No |
| Pin a broadcast to the organization page | Yes | No | No |
| Open **Settings**: organization, org page, members and circles, team, messaging, payments | Yes | No | No |
| See payments and any financial information | Yes | Never | No |
| Reply to or register for events, post, vote and answer surveys | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## What admins and managers see

Admins and managers both open the **Admin Dashboard** from **My Organizations** on their Home page.

| Menu item | Admin | Manager |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Everything happening and coming up in the organization | Their circles and events |
| **Org Circles** | Every circle | Their circles |
| **Org Events** | Every event | Their events |
| **Org Members** | The full roster | The members of their circles |
| **Attention** | Everything waiting on you | Items in their circles and events |
| **Broadcast** | Any circle | Their circles |
| **Settings** | Yes | Not shown |
| **View as member** | Yes | Yes |

## How someone becomes an admin

You can't invite an admin directly. **Everyone starts as a manager**, and an admin promotes them when they're ready.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite them as a manager">
    Open **Settings › Team** and tap **Add Manager**. Enter their first name (last name is optional) and a primary contact, a phone number or an email. You can add a recovery contact too. They get an invitation from your organization and accept it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign their circles and events">
    In **Settings › Team**, tap **⋮** next to the manager, then **Assign manager**. Pick the **Circles** and **One off events** they'll run, then tap **Assign Manager**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make them an admin, when you're ready">
    Tap **⋮** next to the manager, then **Make admin**. They now see every circle, event, member, setting and payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Managers are always invited to the role, even if they're already members of your organization or already use Fast Party.

## Admins

Admins have full access to every circle, event, member and setting, and they're the only people who see payments. The first admin is set up with your organization by the Fast Party team.

- An organization always has **at least one admin**. The last admin can't leave; email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai) to hand over.
- An account can be the admin of **only one** organization. It can still be a member, or a manager, in others.

## Remove a manager

In **Settings › Team**, tap **⋮** next to the manager, then **Remove**. They lose the role but stay a member of your organization.

## Leaving a role

An admin or manager can step down. If they're the only person running something, they're asked to hand it over first.
