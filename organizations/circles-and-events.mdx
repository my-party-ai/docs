Inside an organization, every member is first added to one main circle, **All Members**: your organization's master roster. From there, members are placed in the other circles they need, which different managers can run. Most events happen inside a circle; events for the whole organization are **one-off events**.

## All Members: your master roster

Every organization is created with a main circle called **All Members**. It's the master roster of your organization.

- **Everyone starts here.** Members join your organization by being added to All Members. Anyone you add straight into another circle is added to All Members too, so the roster is always complete.
- **Other circles are drawn from it.** Place members from All Members into the circles they need: a batch, a team, a committee, a chapter. Each circle can be run by a different manager.
- **Rename it, never delete it.** Change its name in **Settings › Members and circles** (for example, "All Alumni" or "All Employees"). It can't be deleted or replaced.
- **One broadcast reaches everyone.** A broadcast to All Members goes to your whole organization.
- **It can't be left on its own.** Leaving All Members means leaving the organization.

## Add members

Admins add members to All Members from **Org Members** on the Admin Dashboard: tap **Add Member**. Managers can add members to the circles they run. For now, members are added by your team; there's no sign-up form yet.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Add New Member** | Add one person by name and phone number or email. |
| **Upload CSV** | Add many people at once from a file. |
| **Add from Org** | Place people who are already in your organization into this circle. |

**Members accept once.** Someone new gets one invitation from your organization and accepts it to join. They land on your [organization page](/organizations/org-page). After that, placing them in more circles never asks them to accept again.

<Tip>
  Set up your [WhatsApp and email](/notifications/your-channels) before you add members, so their invitation comes from your organization.
</Tip>

## The roster: Org Members

**Org Members** is your organization's master roster. Each person shows with their contact, role and status, and the counts at the top show who's on the roster, whose invitations are pending, and who isn't in any circle besides All Members.

- **Place people in a circle:** tick them, pick the circle, then tap **Add to circle**.
- **Take someone out of a circle:** pick that circle in the list, tick them, then tap **Remove**. They stay in your organization.
- **Remove someone from the organization:** pick **All Members** in the list, tick them, then tap **Remove**. That takes them out of the organization and every circle. You're asked to confirm first. Only admins can do this.

Other members never see anyone's phone number or email address.

## Create a circle

Admins and managers can create circles. A manager runs the circles they create.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Create Circle">
    From the Admin Dashboard, tap **Create Circle**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Up to 30 characters, such as `Batch of 2010`, `Golf` or `Hyderabad Chapter`. A description and image are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Add people from your organization with **Add from Org**, or add new people, who join your organization at the same time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To have a manager run the circle, assign it to them in **Settings › Team** (**⋮ › Assign manager**). [Admins and managers →](/organizations/admins-and-managers)

## Leaving a circle

Members can leave any circle except All Members from that circle's page (**⋮ › Exit Circle**). They stay in your organization and on your roster.

## Create events inside a circle

From any circle in your organization, create any of the three event types. Open Events are available only in organizations:

| Type | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| [**RSVP Event**](/private-events/event-creation) | You want to know who's coming. |
| [**Registration Event**](/public-events/overview) | People register, with or without your approval, and may pay for tickets. |
| [**Open Event**](/events/open-events) | Everyone in the circle is welcome, with no RSVP. Open Events are shared only with the circle's members. |

A circle event reaches every member of the circle when you make it live. Once an event is live, it can't be made not live again, so check the details first.

Events in your organization's circles belong to the organization automatically. They work like any other event: set them up on the Event Page, switch on the tools you need, and run them from the Event Dashboard.

## Create a one-off event

Some events belong to the whole organization rather than one circle: an annual gala, Foundation Day or an AGM. Create these with **Create Event** on the Admin Dashboard. They can be RSVP or Registration Events, and people are invited to them one by one.

## See every circle and event

- **Org Circles** lists every circle in your organization.
- **Org Events** lists every event, one-off and circle events. Filter by circle, Live or Not live, event source, event type, date and your role.

## Keep circles active between events

Every circle has a feed where you can share **posts**, run quick **polls**, and send **surveys**. Surveys are drafted for you from a short description of what you want to ask. You review and edit the questions, then publish. [Circles →](/recurring-events/overview)
