Organizations are set up by the Fast Party team, on our paid plans. Once yours is ready, you're its first **admin**, and you set everything up in one place: **Settings** on the Admin Dashboard.

## Request your organization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell us about your organization">
    On your Home page, tap **Set up an organization**. Add your organization's name and type, your name, a phone number or email, and anything else we should know, then tap **Talk to Fast Party**. You can also email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai).
  </Step>
  <Step title="We get in touch">
    We reply within a business day, talk through what you need, and set the organization up with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your Admin Dashboard">
    Once it's ready, your organization appears under **My Organizations** on your Home page. Tap it to open the [Admin Dashboard](/organizations/dashboard).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  An account can be the admin of only one organization. It can still be a member of others.
</Note>

## Settings

Open **Settings** from the Admin Dashboard menu: "Everything about your organization in one place." **Only admins see Settings**; managers don't have it in their menu. Settings has six tabs, and each one saves on its own. Work through them in order:

| # | Tab | What you do there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Organization** | Your logo, time zone and business type. [Below ↓](#organization-details) |
| 2 | **Org page** | Build the page your members and visitors see, and choose who sees each item. [Set up your org page →](/organizations/org-page) |
| 3 | **Members and circles** | Name your main circle, **All Members**, your master roster, and choose whether members see member counts and lists. [Members and circles →](/organizations/members-and-circles) |
| 4 | **Team** | Invite managers, assign them circles and events, and promote admins. [Admins and managers →](/organizations/admins-and-managers) |
| 5 | **Messaging** | Request your organization's own WhatsApp Business number and email sender. [Messaging →](/notifications/your-channels) |
| 6 | **Payments** | Connect your Razorpay (India) or Stripe (USA) account for tickets and contributions. [Payments →](/organizations/payments) |

<Tip>
  **Request messaging early.** WhatsApp and email templates need approval, which can take a few days. Tap **Request setup** in **Settings › Messaging** on day one, then carry on with the other tabs while you wait.
</Tip>

## Organization details

**Settings › Organization** is who you are, used across your pages, invitations and messages.

| Field | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Logo** | Tap **Change logo**. Shown on your organization page, circles, invitations and messages. |
| **Organization Name** | Set when the organization was created. It can't be changed; email us if it needs correcting. |
| **Link name** | The end of your organization page's address, such as `app.fastparty.ai/o/cbit-alumni`. Share it so people can find you. Set when the organization was created and can't be changed. |
| **Region** | India (payouts via Razorpay) or USA (payouts via Stripe). Set when the organization was created and can't be changed; it decides how [payments](/organizations/payments) work. |
| **Time zone** | Used for event times and scheduled messages. |
| **Business type** | Your organization's type, which sets the Fast Party edition you run as. The edition shows in the top bar with your organization's name, for example "Pavilion by Fast Party". |

### Business types

| Business type | Edition | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default | Fast Party | [How it works](/get-started/how-it-works) |
| Schools & Colleges | Reunion by Fast Party | [Reunion](/get-started/reunion) |
| Associations | Quorum by Fast Party | [Quorum](/get-started/quorum) |
| Clubs | Pavilion by Fast Party | [Pavilion](/get-started/pavilion) |
| Corporates | Crew by Fast Party | [Crew](/get-started/crew) |
| Event Planners | Jubila by Fast Party | [Jubila](/get-started/jubila) |
| Venues | Grandstand by Fast Party | [Grandstand](/get-started/grandstand) |

Whatever the type, every organization starts with its All Members roster, and circles are always called circles.
