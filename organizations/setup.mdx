Organizations are set up by the Fast Party team, on our paid plans. Once yours is ready, you're its first **admin**, and everything else lives in one place: **Settings** on the Admin Dashboard.

## Request your organization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell us about your organization">
    On your Home page, tap **Set up an organization**. Add your organization's name and type, your name, a phone number or email, and anything else we should know, then tap **Talk to Fast Party**. You can also email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai).
  </Step>
  <Step title="We get in touch">
    We reply within a business day, talk through what you need, and set the organization up with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your Admin Dashboard">
    Once it's ready, your organization appears under **My Organizations** on your Home page. Tap it to open the [Admin Dashboard](/organizations/dashboard).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  An account can be the admin of only one organization. It can still be a member of others.
</Note>

## Settings

Open **Settings** from the Admin Dashboard menu. **Only admins see Settings**; managers don't have it in their menu. Settings has six tabs, and each one saves on its own.

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization** | Your logo, time zone and business type. The organization's name, link name and region are set when it's created and can't be changed. [Organization details ↓](#organization-details) |
| **Org page** | Everything on your [organization page](/organizations/org-page): banner, tagline, about, address, website, contact WhatsApp and email, social pages, links and documents, and office bearers. Each item is shown to the public, to members only, or hidden. Changes stay in a draft until you tap **Publish**. [Set up your organization page →](/organizations/org-page#set-up-your-organization-page) |
| **Members and circles** | The name of your main circle (**All Members** by default, up to 30 characters). Everyone in your organization belongs to this circle: it's your master roster. Here you also choose **Hide member count and member lists**. [All Members →](/organizations/circles-and-events#all-members-your-master-roster) |
| **Team** | Your admins and managers. Tap **Add Manager** to invite someone, then use the **⋮** menu beside them to **Assign manager** to circles or one-off events, **Make admin**, or **Remove**. [Admins and managers →](/organizations/admins-and-managers) |
| **Messaging** | Your organization's own WhatsApp Business number and email sender. Tap **Request setup** for each; it shows **Request pending** until our team has connected it with you. [Your WhatsApp & Email →](/notifications/your-channels) |
| **Payments** | Your Razorpay (India) or Stripe (USA) business account for tickets and contributions. [Payments ↓](#payments) |

<Tip>
  To see your organization page the way a member does, tap **View as member** in the Admin Dashboard menu. A **Member View** banner reminds you that you're previewing.
</Tip>

## Set things up in this order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Messaging first">
    Members only ever hear from your organization's own WhatsApp number and email, never from Fast Party. In **Settings › Messaging**, tap **Request setup** for WhatsApp and for email, and our team connects them with you. Approval can take a few days, so start early.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your organization page">
    Fill in **Settings › Org page** and tap **Publish**, so new members find something good when they land.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payments, if you collect money">
    Add your Razorpay account in **Settings › Payments** before you sell tickets or raise contributions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Team, members and circles">
    Add your members to **All Members**, invite managers in **Settings › Team** and promote any who should be admins, then build your circles from the roster. [Circles and events →](/organizations/circles-and-events)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Organization details

| Field | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Logo** | Shown on your organization page, circles, invitations and messages. |
| **Organization name** | Set when the organization is created. It can't be changed here; email us if it needs correcting. |
| **Link name** | The end of your organization page's address, such as `app.fastparty.ai/o/cbit-alumni`. Share it so people can find you. Set when the organization is created and can't be changed. |
| **Region** | India (payouts via Razorpay) or USA (payouts via Stripe). Set when the organization is created; it decides how payments work. |
| **Time zone** | Used for event times and scheduled messages. |
| **Business type** | Your organization's type. It sets which Fast Party edition you see and the words used across your dashboard. See the table below. |

## Payments

Tickets and contributions go straight into your organization's own account. Fast Party never holds your money, and only admins see payments.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="India (Razorpay)">
    In **Settings › Payments**, tap **Add Razorpay Account** and set up your business account. You'll need:

    - Your legal organization name and address
    - PAN, and GSTIN if you're registered for GST
    - A contact person's name, email and phone
    - Your bank account details
    - The signed agreement

    Our team then links your Razorpay account so payments settle directly to you.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="USA (Stripe)">
    Payments settle through your organization's own Stripe account. Our team connects it with you during setup.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
