Open **Settings › Payments** on the Admin Dashboard. This is where you connect the account your organization gets paid into. Only admins see it, and only admins ever see payments or any financial information.

**Fast Party never holds your money.** Ticket sales and contributions go straight into your organization's own account.

## What payments are for

- **Tickets** for [Registration Events](/public-events/overview) with paid tiers
- **Contributions** your organization raises from members

Set up payments before you sell tickets or raise contributions.

## Connect your account

Your organization's region decides how payments work. It's set when the organization is created and shown in **Settings › Organization**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="India (Razorpay)">
    Under **Payout**, tap **Add Razorpay Account** and set up your business account. You'll need:

    - Your legal organization name and address
    - PAN, and GSTIN if you're registered for GST
    - A contact person's name, email and phone
    - Your bank account details
    - The signed agreement

    Our team then links your Razorpay account so payments settle directly to you.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="USA (Stripe)">
    Payments settle through your organization's own Stripe account. Our team connects it with you during setup.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Messaging costs (WhatsApp and email) are billed to your organization separately, at cost. [Messaging →](/notifications/your-channels#what-it-costs)
</Note>
