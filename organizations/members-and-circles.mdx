Open **Settings › Members and circles** on the Admin Dashboard. This tab sets how your membership is organized and what members can see of each other. Only admins see it.

## Your main circle: All Members

Every organization is created with one main circle. It's called **All Members** to start with, and it's your organization's **master roster**.

- **Everyone belongs to it.** Every member of your organization is added to the main circle by default, whether you add them to it directly or straight into another circle.
- **Other circles are drawn from it.** Add members from the roster to the circles they need: a batch, a team, a committee, a chapter. Each circle can be run by a different manager. [Circles and events →](/organizations/circles-and-events)
- **Rename it, never delete it.** Change the **Name** to suit your organization, up to 30 characters, then tap **Save**. For example, "All Alumni", "All Club Members" or "All Employees". The main circle can't be deleted or replaced.
- **Leaving it means leaving the organization.** A member can leave any other circle and stay in your organization.

## Hide member count and member lists

Turn this on and members no longer see how many people are in your organization or its circles, or who they are. Tap **Save** to apply it.

| Setting | What members see |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** | The number of circles and members, and who's in each circle they belong to |
| **On** | No counts and no member lists |

Admins and managers still see the full roster on **Org Members**. Other members never see anyone's phone number or email address, whichever you choose.
