An organization brings your circles, members and events together in one place. It's built for groups that run many communities and many events: alumni associations, clubs and societies, event planners and venues.

## What's inside an organization

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Circles** | The groups inside your organization, such as batches, sections, committees or audiences. Each circle has its own members, posts, polls, surveys and events. |
| **Events** | Events inside a circle, plus one-off events that belong to the organization but not to any one circle. |
| **People** | Admins and managers who run the organization, and the members in its circles. |
| **Organization Dashboard** | One view of every circle and event, with a list of what needs your attention. |
| **Broadcasts** | Messages to the members of the circles you choose. |

## Your workspace, ready to go

Your organization is more than a list of events. It's a **workspace**: your members, circles, events, messages and history, all in one place and under your brand.

[Planning with AI](/platforms#connect-your-ai) works everywhere in Fast Party, for a single event or a circle too. In an organization it reaches across the whole workspace: every circle, event and broadcast. Connect Claude or ChatGPT and there's nothing else to install or configure. Open a chat and start asking:

- "Create a circle for the 2015 batch and invite these 40 alumni."
- "Set up our annual meet as a registered event for 300 people, with approval."
- "Which circles have been quiet this month?"
- "Draft a broadcast to all sections about Sunday's AGM."

Your AI works inside your workspace with the same permissions you have in Fast Party. [Connect your AI →](/platforms#connect-your-ai)

## Who does what

The person who creates the organization becomes its first **admin**. Admins invite other admins and managers.

- **Admins** have full access to every circle and event in the organization.
- **Managers** manage only the circles and events they create or are assigned to.

[Admins and managers →](/organizations/admins-and-managers)

<Note>
  A person can belong to only one organization at a time. To create or join a different one, they first exit their current organization.
</Note>
