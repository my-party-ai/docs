An organization brings your members, circles and events together in one place, under your own name. It's built for groups that run many communities and many events: alumni associations, clubs, associations, corporates, event planners and venues.

## How an organization fits together

<Steps>
  <Step title="One member list: All Members">
    Every organization starts with one main circle, **All Members**, created for you. It's your organization's **master roster**: everyone who joins is added to it by default. You can rename it in **Settings › Members and circles** (for example, "All Alumni"), but it can't be deleted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Circles drawn from it">
    Create circles for your batches, chapters, teams or committees, and add members to them from the roster as they need. Each circle can be run by a different manager. Anyone in any of your circles is also in All Members, so your master roster is always complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Events in circles, or for everyone">
    Run events inside a circle, or create **one-off events** that belong to the whole organization, such as an annual gala or an AGM.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's inside an organization

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization page** | Your organization's own page at `app.fastparty.ai/o/your-link-name`. Members see their events, circles and What's new there; visitors see who you are. [The organization page →](/organizations/org-page) |
| **Circles** | The groups inside your organization. Each circle has its own members, posts, polls, surveys and events. |
| **Events** | Events inside a circle, plus one-off events that belong to the whole organization. |
| **People** | Admins and managers who run the organization, and the members on your roster. |
| **Admin Dashboard** | Where admins and managers run the organization: Dashboard, Org Circles, Org Events, Org Members, Attention and Broadcast, plus Settings for admins and **View as member**. [Admin Dashboard →](/organizations/dashboard) |

## Who does what

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Runs everything: every circle and event, the roster, broadcasts, settings and payments. Only admins see financial information. |
| **Manager** | Runs the circles and events they create or are assigned. Managers never see money or Settings. |
| **Member** | Belongs to the organization and its circles, replies to or registers for events, and takes part in posts, polls and surveys. |

An organization always has at least one admin. You can't invite an admin directly: invite someone as a manager in **Settings › Team**, then promote them with **Make admin**. [Admins and managers →](/organizations/admins-and-managers)

<Note>
  A person can be a member of many organizations, but an admin of only one.
</Note>

## How members join

For now, admins and managers add members; there's no sign-up form. A new member accepts **once**, joining your organization and its **All Members** roster, and lands on your organization page. After that they're never asked to accept again when you add them to more circles.

Leaving a circle doesn't take anyone out of your organization. [Circles and events →](/organizations/circles-and-events)

## Plan with your AI

[Planning with AI](/platforms#connect-your-ai) works everywhere in Fast Party. In an organization it reaches across every circle, event and broadcast you can see. Connect Claude or ChatGPT and start asking:

- "Create a circle for the 2015 batch."
- "Set up our annual meet as a Registration Event for 300 people, with approval."
- "Which circles have been quiet this month?"
- "Draft a broadcast to all circles about Sunday's AGM."

Your AI works with the same permissions you have in Fast Party. [Connect your AI →](/platforms#connect-your-ai)

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask us to set up your organization">
    Organizations are set up by the Fast Party team. Request one from your Home page, or email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai). We create it and make you its first admin. [Set up your organization →](/organizations/setup)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp and email">
    Members only ever hear from your organization, so set up your messaging channels first. [Your WhatsApp & Email →](/notifications/your-channels)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your organization page">
    Add your banner, about, contact details and links, and choose what visitors and members see. [The organization page →](/organizations/org-page)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team and members">
    Add your members to All Members, invite managers (and promote any who should be admins), then build your circles from the roster. [Admins and managers →](/organizations/admins-and-managers)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run events and stay in touch">
    Create events, keep an eye on Attention, and reach members with [broadcasts](/organizations/broadcasts).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Organizations are part of our paid plans, with everything included. We can also bring your existing records across in a one-time [data migration](/data-migration).
</Note>
