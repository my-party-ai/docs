A **Registration event** asks you to register. Once you're in, you get a **QR pass** — that's what's scanned at the door.

## How it works

Some events let you straight in; others need the host to approve you first. The event page tells you which.

| | No approval needed | The host approves registrations |
|---|---|---|
| You | Register (and pay, if your tier is paid) | Request a place and answer the host's question |
| Then | Your QR pass appears straight away | You wait for the host to approve you |
| Payment | When you register | After you're approved |
| QR pass | Right away — or once you've paid | Once approved — and paid, for a paid tier |
| Bringing others | Add them as you register; each gets a pass | Each person requests their own place |

## 1. Open the invitation and choose your tier

Tap the link you were sent. Under **Choose your tier**, pick the kind of place you want — for example General or VIP. Each tier shows how many seats are left and whether it's free or paid. You'll also see any age limit and when registration closes.

{/* Screenshot to add: images/guests/choose-your-tier.png — Choose your tier, an age notice, a tier with seats left and Free Entry, the closing date and Register */}

Tap **Register**.

## 2. Confirm your details

Check your name, phone number and recovery email.

- **If the host approves registrations**, answer the host's question — it helps them decide — then tap **Request to join**.
- **If there's no approval**, you can add people coming with you: under **Add people coming with you**, tap **Add Attendees** and enter each person's name (a phone number or email is optional). Each person gets their own QR pass.

If the event has an age limit, tick the box to confirm, then tap **Register**.

## 3. Pay, if your tier is paid

Paid tiers take you to payment on the next step — straight away with no approval, or after the host approves you.

## 4. Get your QR pass

**No approval:** your pass appears on the event page as soon as you've registered (and paid, if needed).

**With approval:** you'll see **Pending Approval** until the host reviews your request. You'll be notified once you're approved, and your QR pass will appear on the event page.

{/* Screenshot to add: images/guests/pending-approval.png — Pending Approval, your request has been submitted */}

See [Your QR pass](/guests/your-qr-pass) for what to do with it.

## 5. Check the event tools and host updates

Once you're registered, the event page opens the host's tools to you. Tools marked **Action needed**, like your food choices or travel details, are waiting on you. Host updates are at the bottom of the page. See [Your event page](/guests/your-event-page).

## When you can't register

If registration has closed, is paused, or the event is full, the event page says so.

## Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which tier should I pick?">
    It's your choice — compare what each tier includes, its price and seats left. The host's notes on each tier are on the page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I answer to the host's question?">
    Whatever's true for you — your answer goes straight to the host and helps them decide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I register friends too?">
    On events without approval, yes — add them under **Add people coming with you**, and each person gets a QR pass. On events the host approves, each person requests their own place.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
    Check the event's description. If it doesn't say, contact the host.
  </Accordion>
