An **RSVP event** asks one thing of you: will you be joining? You'll usually get the invitation on WhatsApp or by email.

## 1. Open the invitation

Tap **View Invitation** in the message. It opens the event page — the date, time, venue (with **Get Directions**), who's hosting, and what the event is about.

## 2. Reply Yes, Maybe or No

Your reply card is on the event page.

{/* Screenshot to add: images/guests/reply-card.png — the reply card: You're Invited – will you be joining? Yes, Maybe or No */}

You can change your reply any time later, so pick what's true right now.

## 3. Tell the host who's coming with you

When you pick **Yes** or **Maybe**, the card asks **Anyone coming with you?** Add the number of **Adults** and **Children** coming with you — not counting yourself — then tap **Confirm**.

{/* Screenshot to add: images/guests/anyone-coming-with-you.png — Anyone coming with you? with Adults and Children, and Confirm */}

The card then shows your reply and the total, including you.

{/* Screenshot to add: images/guests/rsvpd-yes.png — You've RSVP'd Yes, 4 guests including you, with Change RSVP */}

## Change your reply

Tap **Change RSVP** on your reply card and pick again. If you're coming after all, choose **Yes** or **Maybe** and add who's coming with you.

<Warning>
When you change your reply, check the Adults and Children counts before you tap **Confirm** — they may start again at 0.
</Warning>

## What opens once you reply

Saying **Yes** or **Maybe** opens the tools the host has set up for guests who are coming — like choosing what you'd like to eat and drink, your travel details, or what you'll bring. Tools marked **Action needed** are waiting on you.

If you say **No**, you'll still see the host's updates, and you can still send a gift or a message in **Gifting & Best Wishes**.

## Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I bring someone?">
    Yes — add them under **Anyone coming with you?** when you reply Yes or Maybe, or later with **Change RSVP**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a deadline to reply?">
    If the host set one, it's on the event page. Otherwise, reply whenever you're ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a pass for an RSVP event?">
    No. RSVP events don't use QR passes — you're on the host's guest list.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
