A **circle** is a group that keeps in touch and plans its events together, like a family, an alumni batch, a team or a club. An organizer invites you, you join, and from then on you see the circle's posts and get invited to its events.

## Join the circle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation">
    You get the invitation on WhatsApp, by email, or as a notification in Fast Party. Open the link. You don't need the app; it works in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check who it's from">
    The page shows the circle's name and who it's **Managed by**. It also shows the phone number or email you're joining with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Join the circle">
    That's it. You're a member, and the circle page opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the number or email under **Joining as** isn't yours, don't join. Contact the organizer and ask them to invite the right one.
</Note>

## What you see as a member

| On the circle page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Posts & Polls** | Updates, polls and surveys from the organizers |
| **Events** | The circle's events you've been invited to |
| **Members** | Who else is in the circle, unless the organizer has hidden the list |

### Posts, polls and surveys

- **Posts**: updates from the organizers. Tap **Like** on the ones you like. Only organizers can post.
- **Polls**: tap an option to vote.
- **Surveys**: a short form from the organizers. Your answers go to the organizers.
- **Contribute**: if a post is collecting money for a cause, tap **Contribute** to pay. The organizers see who contributed and how much.

## The circle's events

When an organizer makes an event live, every member is **invited automatically**. There's nothing to sign up for. You get the invitation like any other event, then reply or register on the event itself. See [Reply to an invitation](/guests/reply-to-an-invitation) or [Register for an event](/guests/register-for-an-event).

Events the organizers are still planning don't show up for you. You'll be invited when they go live.

## Ask Antsy

On the circle page, tap **Ask Antsy** in the bottom-right corner to ask about the circle, like "What's new?" or "Is there a poll I haven't answered?". Antsy only knows what's in this circle, and it never votes, posts or pays for you.

## Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I invite someone to the circle?">
    No. Only organizers can add members. Ask an organizer to invite them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't see the member list">
    The organizer has chosen to keep it private. You can still take part in everything else.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I leave a circle?">
    Open the **⋮** menu next to the circle's name and tap **Exit Circle**. You'll stop seeing its posts and won't be invited to its new events.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
