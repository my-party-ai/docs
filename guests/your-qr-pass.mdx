Your **QR pass** is what gets you in. It's scanned at the door to check you in.

## Where it is

Your pass is on the event page, under **You're registered**, with one pass for each person you registered. You'll also get a confirmation message when it's ready.

{/* Screenshot to add: images/guests/qr-passes.png — You're registered, the tier and two QR passes, with download and share icons */}

## Save it

Tap the **download** icon to save your passes as a PDF — handy if you won't have signal at the venue.

## Share it

Tap the **share** icon to send the passes as a PDF — to your own phone, or to the people you registered so they have theirs.

<Note>
Sharing sends every pass in your order in one PDF. Passes are in each person's name and can't be transferred to someone else.
</Note>

## At the door

Show your QR pass — on your phone or printed — and it's scanned when you arrive. If it can't be scanned, the host can check you in by hand.

## Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't find my pass">
    It's always on the event page in the app, under You're registered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see a pass — I'm waiting">
    If the host approves registrations, your pass appears once you're approved (and paid, for a paid tier). Until then you'll see Pending Approval.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I give my pass to a friend?">
    No — each pass is in the name of the person it was issued to.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
