Someone has invited you to something on Fast Party. What you do next depends on the kind of invitation.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Reply to an invitation" icon="reply" href="/guests/reply-to-an-invitation">
    The invitation asks **Yes, Maybe or No**. Reply, and tell the host who's coming with you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Register for an event" icon="ticket" href="/guests/register-for-an-event">
    The invitation asks you to **register**. Pick a tier, register, and get your QR pass.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your QR pass" icon="qrcode" href="/guests/your-qr-pass">
    Where your pass lives, how to save or share it, and showing it at the door.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Being in a circle" icon="users" href="/guests/being-in-a-circle">
    Posts, polls and surveys, contributing to a cause, and the circle's events.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Which one is mine?

| Your invitation says | It's | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| "will you be joining?" with Yes, Maybe, No | An RSVP event | [Reply to an invitation](/guests/reply-to-an-invitation) |
| "Choose your tier" and Register | A Registration event | [Register for an event](/guests/register-for-an-event) |
| You've been invited to join a circle | A circle | [Being in a circle](/guests/being-in-a-circle) |

<Tip>
On the event page, tap **Ask Antsy** in the bottom-right corner whenever you have a question about the event. Antsy only knows what's on that event, and it never replies, registers or pays for you.
</Tip>
