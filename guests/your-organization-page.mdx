When a club, association, school or company adds you on Fast Party, its invitation brings you to its **organization page**. This is your home for everything from that organization: who they are, your events, the circles you're in, their news and how to reach them.

You accept the organization's invitation **once**. After that, when they add you to more circles or events, you're never asked to join again.

## Your first five minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look at My Circles">
    These are the groups you're in. Everyone is in the main circle (usually **All Members**); your organization may also put you in smaller circles, such as a team, a batch or an interest group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check My Events">
    Anything the organization has invited you to is here, with your reply or registration status. If it's empty, nothing has been scheduled for you yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read About and What's new">
    About is the organization in its own words. What's new is where it posts news for members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note how to reach them">
    **Contact us** has the organization's contact details. Use it for questions, or if you want to leave.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask Antsy anything">
    Tap **Ask Antsy** in the bottom-right corner. It knows this page and your own events and circles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll find on the page

| Part | What's in it | What to do there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Header** | The organization's logo, name, tagline and how many circles it has. Buttons such as **Website** or **Email** if it shares them. | Tap a button to visit their website or write to them. |
| **About** | What the organization is about. | Read it to get to know them. |
| **Links and documents** | Forms, bylaws, brochures, policies and other resources. | Tap one to open it. |
| **My Events** | Your events from the organization, with your status. Arrows scroll; **View all** shows the full list. | Tap an event to reply or register. |
| **My Circles** | The circles you're in. | Tap one for its posts, polls, surveys and events. |
| **Contact us** | Website, email, WhatsApp, address, and **Follow us** with their social pages. | Reach the organization directly. |
| **Office bearers** | The people who run the organization, with their titles. | See who's who. |
| **What's new** | News the organization has posted for members. | Check back for updates. |
| **Go to my home** | At the bottom of the page. | Opens your own Fast Party Home. |

Each organization chooses what goes on its page, so some parts may be missing or empty. An empty part simply means the organization hasn't added anything there yet.

## What to expect

- **Invitations come from the organization.** When it schedules something for you, you get an invitation on WhatsApp or by email from the organization's own number or address, and the event appears under **My Events**.
- **No app needed.** Everything works in your browser from the link you're sent. The Fast Party app for iPhone and Android is there if you'd like it. [Get the app →](/platforms)
- **Replying and registering** happen on each event's page. [Reply to an invitation →](/guests/reply-to-an-invitation) · [Register for an event →](/guests/register-for-an-event)
- **Circles** have their own posts, polls and surveys, and their own events. [Being in a circle →](/guests/being-in-a-circle)

## Getting back here

- On any page, tap the **organization's name** in the top bar.
- On your Home, open it under **My Organizations**.
- Or tap the link in any message from the organization.

The **Fast Party** logo at the top left always takes you to your own Home.

## Your details

Other members never see your phone number or email address. The organization's admins and managers can, so they can reach you. The organization may also hide member counts and member lists.

## Leaving

- **A circle:** open the circle, tap **⋮**, then **Exit Circle**. You stay in the organization.
- **The organization:** contact it through **Contact us**.

## Your own events

Fast Party is yours too. **Go to my home** takes you to your own Home, where you can plan birthdays, trips and other personal events, or start your own circles. What you create there is yours, not the organization's.

<Tip>
  Not sure where something is? Tap **Ask Antsy** and ask, for example, "Which circles am I in?", "How will I hear about events?" or "How do I get back here?"
</Tip>
