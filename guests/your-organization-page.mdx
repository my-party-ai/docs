When an organization adds you as a member, its invitation brings you to the **organization page**. It's the one place you see everything from that organization: who they are, your events, your circles and their announcements.

You accept the organization's invitation **once**. After that, when they add you to more circles, you're never asked to accept again.

## What's on the page

| Part | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Header** | The organization's logo, name and tagline, how many circles it has, and a **Website** button if it has one. |
| **About** | What the organization is about, in its own words. |
| **Contact us** | Its website, contact WhatsApp and email, address, and **Follow us** with its social pages. |
| **My Events** | Your events from the organization, with your reply or registration status. Use the arrows to scroll, or **View all** for the full list. |
| **My Circles** | The circles you're in. Tap one to open it. |
| **Announcements** | Messages the organization has pinned for members. |
| **Links and documents** | Forms, bylaws, brochures and other resources the organization shares. |
| **Office bearers** | The people who run the organization, if it shows them. |

Each organization chooses what goes on its page, so some parts may not appear.

## Your events and circles

Tap an event to open its page, then reply or register there. [Reply to an invitation →](/guests/reply-to-an-invitation) · [Register for an event →](/guests/register-for-an-event)

Tap a circle to see its posts, polls, surveys and events. [Being in a circle →](/guests/being-in-a-circle)

## Your details

Other members never see your phone number or email address. The organization's admins and managers can, so they can reach you. The organization may also hide member counts and lists from members.

## Leaving

- **A circle:** open the circle, tap **⋮**, then **Exit Circle**. You stay in the organization.
- **The organization:** get in touch through **Contact us** on the organization page.

## Your own events

Fast Party is yours too. **Go to my home** at the bottom of the page takes you to your own Home, where you can plan your own events and start your own circles.

<Tip>
  Tap **Ask Antsy** in the bottom-right corner to ask what's on the page, where your events are, or how to leave a circle.
</Tip>
