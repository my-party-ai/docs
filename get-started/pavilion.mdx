**The home of your club community.** Pavilion by Fast Party is Fast Party set up for clubs: sports, social, country, riding, golf and polo clubs, and any club with members, facilities and a calendar. Your member register lives in one place, every circle is built from it, and every notice goes out once, from the club.

## How a club fits together

| In Fast Party | For your club |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Your club, with its own [organization page](/organizations/org-page) |
| All Members | Your member register: everyone in the club. You can rename it, for example "All Club Members". |
| Circles | Circles drawn from the register, for each sport, interest group or committee |
| Events | Club nights, tournaments, chukkers, AGMs and family days |
| Managers | The people who run a circle or an event for you |

## Get set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your organization with our team">
    Tap **Set up an organization** on your Home page, or email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai). We set up your organization with your logo, your own WhatsApp Business number and email sender, and payments into your own Razorpay (India) or Stripe (US) account. [Set up your organization →](/organizations/setup)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring your register across">
    Send us your member register and past event records. We import them once into **All Members**, so you start with your history in place. Where family members share a phone number, the roster keeps one row per person. [How data migration works →](/data-migration)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your circles">
    A member joins the club once, into All Members, and lands on the club's organization page. Placing them in a circle for a sport, a committee or a group is a pick from the register, not another invitation. [Circles and events →](/organizations/circles-and-events)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first club event">
    Run a club night, a tournament, an AGM or a family day. Use a [Registration Event](/public-events/overview) for sign-ups and tickets with QR check-in at the gate, an [RSVP Event](/private-events/event-creation) for an invitation-only gathering, or an [Open Event](/events/open-events) when every member of a circle is welcome and you don't need RSVPs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send notices to the right circles">
    Send one broadcast to All Members or to exactly the circles you choose. Each member receives it only once, even if they belong to several circles. [Broadcasts →](/organizations/broadcasts)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Everything: the roster, every circle and event, broadcasts, Settings and payments. The first admin is set up with your organization; more admins are managers who've been promoted. |
| **Manager** | Runs the circles and events they create or are assigned. Never money or Settings. |
| **Member** | Be part of their circles, RSVP or register for events, and take part in posts, polls and surveys. |

Fees and collections go straight to the club's own account. Fast Party never holds the money.

<Tip>
  Already have a club app? Pavilion can run alongside it, adding Registration Events, notices and the AI connection on top, or it can run everything. Your call.
</Tip>

## Ask your club data anything

[Connect Claude or ChatGPT](/claude-integration/overview) to your club and ask in plain language:

- "Which circle is most active this year?"
- "Who came to last month's club night?"
- "Message the Golf circle about Sunday."

Your AI sees only what your account can see in Fast Party.
