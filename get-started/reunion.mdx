**Bring people back together.** Reunion by Fast Party is Fast Party set up for schools and colleges. It gives your alumni association one master roster, every batch and chapter circle built from it, and every reunion run from one place, under your institution's crest.

## How an alumni association fits together

| In Fast Party | For your association |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Your alumni association, with its own [organization page](/organizations/org-page) |
| All Members | Your master roster: every alum in the association. You can rename it, for example "All Alumni". |
| Circles | Circles drawn from the roster, for each batch, branch, city chapter or interest group |
| Events | Reunions, meets and Foundation Day |
| Managers | Batch and chapter coordinators |

## Get set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your organization with our team">
    Tap **Set up an organization** on your Home page, or email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai). We set up your organization with your crest, your own WhatsApp Business number and email sender, and payments into the institution's Razorpay or Stripe account. [Set up your organization →](/organizations/setup)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring your alumni records across">
    Send us your batch lists and past reunion records, wherever they live. We import them once into **All Members**, so you start with your history in place. [How data migration works →](/data-migration)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your circles">
    An alum joins the association once, into All Members, and lands on its organization page. Placing them in a batch, city chapter or interest group circle is a pick from the roster, not another invitation. Use posts, polls and surveys to keep each circle active between reunions. [Circles and events →](/organizations/circles-and-events)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first reunion">
    Run a batch reunion, a chapter meet or Foundation Day. Use a [Registration Event](/public-events/overview) when alumni sign up or buy tickets, an [RSVP Event](/private-events/event-creation) for an invitation-only gathering, or an [Open Event](/events/open-events) when the whole batch is welcome and you don't need RSVPs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach the right alumni, once">
    Send invitations and appeals to exactly the circles you choose. Each alum receives a message only once, even if they belong to several circles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the day">
    Check alumni in by QR code at the gate, share the day's photos, and send a feedback form afterward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Everything: the roster, every circle and event, broadcasts, Settings and payments. The first admin is set up with your organization; more admins are managers who've been promoted. |
| **Manager** (a batch or chapter coordinator) | Runs the circles and events they create or are assigned: members, posts and events. Never money or Settings. |
| **Alum** | Be part of their circles, RSVP or register for events, and take part in posts, polls and surveys. |

Contributions and registration fees go straight to the institution's own account. Fast Party never holds the money.

## Ask your alumni data anything

[Connect Claude or ChatGPT](/claude-integration/overview) to your association and ask in plain language:

- "Which batch circle is most active this year?"
- "Who hasn't attended a reunion in three years?"
- "Draft the reunion invitation for the 2010 batch."

Your AI sees only what your account can see in Fast Party.
