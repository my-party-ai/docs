Fast Party has three layers. An **organization** holds **circles**, and circles hold **events**. You only use the layers you need: a host planning one birthday needs only an event, while an alumni association uses all three.

## Events

An event is a single occasion with its own page, dashboard and tools. Every event is one of three types.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="RSVP Event" icon="envelope-open-text">
    Invitation-based. You control the guest list, and invited guests RSVP directly. No registration queue, no approvals. Best for birthdays, dinners, weddings, meetings and private functions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Registered Event" icon="ticket">
    Registration-based. You share a registration link and people sign up. When you create the event, you choose whether registrations need your **approval** or people can **register directly**. If the event is priced, attendees pay once their spot is confirmed. Supports ticket types and capacity limits. Best for conferences, reunions, tournaments and ticketed shows.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open Event" icon="door-open">
    No RSVP needed: everyone in the circle is welcome. An Open Event is created inside a circle and shared only with that circle's members. Best for a weekly meetup, a club night or a batch get-together.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Which one do I need?

| If you're running… | Use | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A birthday, dinner, wedding, meeting or private function | **RSVP Event** | You know exactly who's invited and want to know who's coming. |
| A conference, reunion, tournament or ticketed show | **Registered Event** | You need a registration link, capacity and, optionally, ticket sales. Choose approval-required to vet each sign-up, or direct registration to let people in straight away. |
| A gathering where every circle member is welcome and you don't need a headcount | **Open Event** | No invitations or RSVPs to manage. Circle members just see it and come. |
| A series with the same group, such as a monthly book club or an annual meet | **A circle** holding any of the three types | Add members once and reuse them for every event in the series. |

### Event tools

Each event comes with tools you switch on as you need them. Guests see only the tools that apply to them.

| Before the event | On the day | After the event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event Schedule, Event Lineup, Event Links, Food & Beverage Preferences, Potluck, Tasks, Travel & Stay, Event Folder | Check-in, Guest ETA, Activity Feed | Photos, Gifting & Best Wishes, Feedback Corner |

## Circles

A circle is a saved group of people, such as a family, a work team, a book club or a college batch. You add members once, then create events inside the circle and invite everyone in one step.

Circles also keep people connected between events with **posts**, **polls** and **surveys**. Any of the three event types can live inside a circle, and Open Events can only be created inside one. [Learn about circles →](/recurring-events/overview)

## Organizations

An organization is the home for an institution, club or business that runs many circles and events. It is built around one **roster**: a member joins the organization once and can then be placed in any circle without a new invitation.

An organization gets:

- **Your brand.** Your logo and colors on every page, invitation and reminder.
- **Your channels.** Messages go out from your own WhatsApp Business number and email sender, at cost.
- **Your payments.** Fees, tickets and contributions go straight into your own Razorpay (India) or Stripe (US) account. Fast Party never holds your money.
- **One dashboard and one broadcast.** See every circle and event at once, and send one message to exactly the circles you choose. Each person receives it only once.
- **Roles that match real life.** Organization admins, managers who run their own circles, and members.

Organizations are part of our paid plans. To set one up, email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai) and our team will get you started. We can also bring your existing records across in a one-time [data migration](/data-migration).

### Your workspace speaks your language

When an organization is set up, it picks its type, and the workspace relabels itself.

| Organization type | Brand | Circles are called | People are called | Events are called |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| School or college | [Reunion](/get-started/reunion) | Batches and chapters | Alumni | Reunions and meets |
| Club or association | [Coterie](/get-started/coterie) | Sections and committees | Members | Club events |
| Event planner or agency | [Jubila](/get-started/jubila) | Guest lists and audiences | Guests | Events and shows |
| Venue or space | [Belvedere](/get-started/belvedere) | Audiences and member groups | Visitors and members | Programs |

It is the same product underneath. Only the words change.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Host** | The person who creates and runs an event. |
| **Co-host** | A guest the host has given host permissions for an event. |
| **Guest** | Someone invited to an RSVP Event. |
| **Attendee** | Someone registered for a Registered Event. |
| **Member** | Someone in a circle or organization. Circle members are the people an Open Event is shared with. |
| **Vendor** | A business working your event, such as a caterer or decorator. Vendors accept your request, then see only the tasks assigned to them. |
| **Collaborator** | Someone outside the event, such as a driver, who is given a single task and sees only that task. |
| **Tools** | The features you switch on for an event, such as Event Schedule or Potluck. |
| **Event Page** | Where you set up an event and what guests see. |
| **Event Dashboard** | Where you run an event: RSVPs, registrations, check-ins, tasks and activity in one view. |
| **Self check-in** | Guests mark themselves as arrived at an RSVP Event. Optional, and turned on by the host. |
| **QR check-in** | Attendees at a Registered Event are checked in by scanning their QR code. |
| **Broadcast** | A message an organization sends to one or more circles at once. |
| **Ask Antsy** | The button on every screen that shows nudges for your events and help videos. |
