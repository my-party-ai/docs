Fast Party has three layers. An **organization** holds **circles**, and circles hold **events**. You only use the layers you need: a host planning one birthday needs only an event, while an alumni association uses all three.

## Events

An event is a single occasion with its own page, dashboard and tools. Every event is one of three types.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="RSVP Event" icon="envelope-open-text" href="/private-events/event-creation">
    Invitation-based. You control the guest list, and invited guests RSVP directly. No registration queue, no approvals. Best for birthdays, dinners, weddings, meetings and private functions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Registration Event" icon="ticket" href="/public-events/overview">
    Registration-based. You share a registration link and people sign up. When you create the event, you choose whether registrations need your **approval** or people can **register directly**. If the event is priced, attendees pay once their spot is confirmed. Supports ticket types and capacity limits. Best for conferences, reunions, tournaments and ticketed shows.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open Event" icon="door-open" href="/events/open-events">
    No RSVP needed: everyone in the circle is welcome. Available in organizations: create it in one of your organization's circles and it's shared only with that circle's members. Best for a weekly meetup, a club night or a batch get-together.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  **Any event can run over several days.** RSVP, Registration and Open Events can all be multi-day, and each day can have its own invitation template, up to 5 per event. [Templates for multi-day events →](/events/banner-and-templates#multi-day-events-a-template-for-each-day)
</Tip>

### Which one do I need?

| If you're running… | Use | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A birthday, dinner, wedding, meeting or private function | **RSVP Event** | You know exactly who's invited and want to know who's coming. |
| A conference, reunion, tournament or ticketed show | **Registration Event** | You need a registration link, capacity and, optionally, ticket sales. Choose approval-required to vet each sign-up, or direct registration to let people in straight away. |
| A gathering where every member of an organization circle is welcome and you don't need a headcount | **Open Event** | No invitations or RSVPs to manage. Circle members just see it and come. Available in organizations. |
| A series with the same group, such as a monthly book club or an annual meet | **A circle** holding RSVP or Registration Events, plus Open Events in an organization | Add members once and reuse them for every event in the series. |

## Circles

A circle is a saved group of people, such as a family, a work team, a book club or a college batch. You add members once, then create events inside the circle and invite everyone in one step.

Circles also keep people connected between events with **posts**, **polls** and **surveys**. RSVP and Registration Events can live inside any circle. Open Events are available only in an organization's circles. [Learn about circles →](/recurring-events/overview)

## Organizations

An organization is the home for an institution, club or business that runs many circles and events. Think of it as your **workspace**: once it's created and connected to Claude or ChatGPT, it's set up and ready to go. It is built around one **roster**: a member joins the organization once and can then be placed in any circle without a new invitation.

An organization gets:

- **Your brand.** Your logo and colors on every page, invitation and reminder.
- **Your channels.** Messages go out from your own WhatsApp Business number and email sender, at cost.
- **Your payments.** Fees, tickets and contributions go straight into your own Razorpay (India) or Stripe (US) account. Fast Party never holds your money.
- **One dashboard and one broadcast.** See every circle and event at once, and send one message to exactly the circles you choose. Each person receives it only once.
- **Roles that match real life.** The person who creates the organization becomes its admin. Admins have full access to every circle and event; managers run only the circles and events they create or are assigned to.

Organizations are part of our paid plans. To set one up, email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai) and our team will get you started. We can also bring your existing records across in a one-time [data migration](/data-migration). [All about organizations →](/organizations/overview)

### Your workspace speaks your language

When an organization is set up, it picks its type, and the workspace relabels itself.

| Organization type | Brand | Circles are called | People are called | Events are called |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Club or association | [Coterie](/get-started/coterie) | Sections and committees | Members | Club events |
| Event planner or agency | [Jubila](/get-started/jubila) | Guest lists and audiences | Guests | Events and shows |

It is the same product underneath. Only the words change.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Host** | The person who creates and runs an event. |
| **Co-host** | A guest the host has given host permissions for an event. |
| **Guest** | Someone invited to an RSVP Event. |
| **Attendee** | Someone registered for a Registration Event. |
| **Member** | Someone in a circle or organization. In an organization, circle members are the people an Open Event is shared with. |
| **Collaborator** | Someone outside the event, such as a driver, who is given a single task and sees only that task. |
| **Event tools** | The optional features you set up for your guests on an event, such as Event Schedule or Potluck Manager. |
| **Event Page** | Where you set up an event and what guests see. |
| **Event Dashboard** | Where you run an event: RSVPs, registrations, check-ins, tasks and activity in one view, and event broadcasts to your guests. |
| **Self check-in** | Guests mark themselves as arrived at an RSVP Event. Optional, and turned on by the host. |
| **QR check-in** | Attendees at a Registration Event are checked in by scanning their QR code. |
| **Broadcast** | One message to many people: from the Event Dashboard to an event's guests, or from an organization to one or more circles. |
| **Ask Antsy** | The button on every page, coming at the end of September, that guides you step by step, answers questions about the page, and takes you where you need to go. |
