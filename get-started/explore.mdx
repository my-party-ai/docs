## Signing in

On the sign-in page, use whichever is easiest:

- **Phone number:** choose your country code, enter your number, tap **Send verification code**, then enter the code.
- **Email:** tap **Use email instead**, enter your email address and the code we send you.
- **Google** or **Microsoft:** one tap with the account you already have.

## Events, circles and organizations

Everything sits in three layers, and you only use the ones you need:

- **Events:** one occasion with its own page, guest list and tools. A birthday, a team offsite, a reunion.
- **Circles:** the same group of people, saved once, for every event you plan together.
- **Organizations:** for alumni associations, clubs, planners and companies that run many circles and events.

[How Fast Party works →](/get-started/how-it-works)

## Switch on only the tools you need

Event Schedule, Guest Preferences, Potluck Manager, Travel & Stay, Tasks, Check-In, Photos, Feedback Corner and more. Each one is off until you turn it on. [All event tools →](/events/event-tools)

## Ask Antsy

Antsy is your guide inside the app. Tap **Ask Antsy** on any screen to learn what that tool does and what to do next. [Meet Antsy →](/features/antsy-ai)

## Plan it in Claude

Click the Claude icon in the top bar of the app (it reads **Connect to Claude**), then just ask:

> "Plan our office Diwali lunch for 60 people on Friday 6 November, and collect everyone's food preferences."

Claude creates the event and sets up the tools. It asks before it sends any invitations. [Connect your AI →](/platforms#connect-your-ai)
