**Your venue's audience and events.** Grandstand by Fast Party is Fast Party set up for spaces that put on programs: museums, galleries, cultural centers, cafés, studios, halls and co-working spaces. It puts your program, your audience and the door on one platform that remembers every night you've run.

## How a venue fits together

| In Fast Party | For your venue |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Your venue, with its own [organization page](/organizations/org-page) |
| All Members | Everyone on your roster. You can rename it, for example "All Members & Regulars". |
| Circles | Circles drawn from the roster, for members, regulars, patrons or each program's audience |
| Events | Programs: openings, talks, gigs, workshops and markets |
| Managers | Program managers who run a circle or an event for you |

## Get set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your organization with our team">
    Tap **Set up an organization** on your Home page, or email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai). We set up your organization with your logo, your own WhatsApp Business number and email sender, and payments into your own Razorpay (India) or Stripe (US) account. [Set up your organization →](/organizations/setup)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring your audience across">
    Send us your ticketing exports, mailing lists and past program records. We import them once into **All Members**, so you start knowing who has already come. [How data migration works →](/data-migration)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your circles">
    Group people in circles from the roster, for members, regulars, patrons or a program's audience. Posts and polls keep them engaged between visits. [Circles and events →](/organizations/circles-and-events)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first program">
    Run an opening, a talk, a gig, a workshop or a pop-up market, free or ticketed. Use a [Registration Event](/public-events/overview) for tickets and sign-ups, with QR check-in at the door, or an [Open Event](/events/open-events) for members-only programs where everyone in the circle is welcome without an RSVP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the room">
    Send a broadcast to exactly the circles you choose. Each visitor receives it only once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Visitors don't need an app. Tickets, check-in and every visitor tool work in the browser from a WhatsApp or email link.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Everything: the roster, every circle and event, broadcasts, Settings and payments. The first admin is set up with your organization; more admins are managers who've been promoted. |
| **Manager** | Runs the circles and events they create or are assigned. Never money or Settings. |
| **Visitor or member** | Register, buy tickets, check in, and take part in posts and polls. |

## Ask your venue data anything

[Connect Claude or ChatGPT](/claude-integration/overview) to your organization and ask in plain language:

- "Which program filled the room fastest?"
- "Who came to three openings this season?"
- "Draft the invitation for Friday's opening."
