This walkthrough creates an RSVP Event, the invitation-based type, and gets your first guests invited. Setting up an organization instead? Start with the guide for [your organization type](/get-started/how-it-works#organization-types).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [app.fastparty.ai](https://app.fastparty.ai) in any browser, or download the app on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fast-party/id6748051479) or [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.fastparty.mobile). Sign in with whichever is easiest:

    - **Phone number:** choose your country code, enter your number, tap **Send verification code**, then enter the code
    - **Email:** tap **Use email instead**, enter your email address and the code we send you
    - **Google** or **Microsoft:** one tap with the account you already have

    It's the same account everywhere, and everything stays in sync. You land on your **Home** page. On desktop, the Fast Party logo at the far left of the top bar always brings you back here. New to Fast Party? [Take the tour →](/get-started/tour)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the event">
    On your Home page, click **Create Event** under **My Events**, and choose **RSVP Event**. Add an event name, a start date and who it's hosted by. Everything else is optional and can be changed later:

    - Add an [event template and banner](/events/banner-and-templates): pick from the template store or upload your own. They're the first thing your guests see.
    - Add a venue, and Fast Party finds it on Google Maps
    - Leave the date for later if you haven't decided yet

    Click **Create Event**. Your Event Page is ready. This is where you set everything up, and it's what your guests see. [All event fields →](/private-events/event-creation)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your guests">
    Click **Invite** on the Event Page. You can:

    - **Share Invite:** share one link on WhatsApp, email or any other channel
    - **Add Guest:** add people by phone number or email, or pick them from people you've invited before
    - **Upload CSV:** add a list of names, emails and phone numbers at once

    Guests receive their invitation by WhatsApp or email, with a personal RSVP link. They don't need the app to respond. [Guests & RSVPs →](/private-events/guests-rsvp)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on the tools you need">
    Add only what your event needs from the **Tools** list on the Event Page. Popular first tools:

    - [Event Schedule](/private-events/event-schedule) so guests know the plan
    - [Guest Preferences](/private-events/food-beverage) to collect dietary needs
    - [Tasks](/private-events/tasks) to assign jobs to co-hosts, guests or helpers
    - [Photos](/private-events/photos) for a shared album on the day
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it from the Event Dashboard">
    Once invitations are out, click **Dashboard** on the Event Page, in a laptop or tablet browser. This is where you run the event: watch RSVPs arrive, send a reminder to anyone who hasn't responded, message all your guests with an event broadcast, and keep track of tasks and check-ins on the day. [Event Dashboard →](/private-events/event-dashboard)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Or do it all in Claude

Prefer to type it than tap it? Connect Fast Party to Claude once, then plan in a chat.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    In the app, click the Claude icon in the top bar (it reads **Connect to Claude**). Fast Party is added to your Claude account. Requires Claude Pro or higher. Using ChatGPT? [See the steps →](/platforms#connect-your-ai)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask">
    In Claude, say something like: "Create a birthday dinner for Saturday at 7 pm at our house, invite my family circle, and set up a potluck." Claude creates the event and sets up the tools, and asks before it sends any invitations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep asking">
    Ask "Who hasn't replied?" or "Which dishes are still unclaimed?" any time. Everything Claude does shows up in the app straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

More ideas: [example prompts](/claude-integration/example-prompts) · [what you can do](/claude-integration/what-you-can-do)
