This walkthrough creates an RSVP Event, the invitation-based type, and gets your first guests invited. Setting up an organization instead? Start with the guide for [your organization type](/get-started/how-it-works#your-workspace-speaks-your-language).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [app.fastparty.ai](https://app.fastparty.ai) in any browser, or [download the app](/platforms) on iOS or Android. It's the same account everywhere, and everything stays in sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the event">
    Click **New Event** and choose **RSVP Event**. Add an event name, a start date and who it's hosted by. Everything else is optional and can be changed later:

    - Pick an image from the template gallery or upload your own
    - Add a venue, and Fast Party finds it on Google Maps
    - Set the date to **TBD** if you haven't decided yet

    Click **Save**. Your event page is ready. [All event fields →](/private-events/event-creation)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your guests">
    Tap the **Invite** icon on the event page. You can:

    - **Share the invite link** on WhatsApp, email or any other channel
    - **Add guests** by phone number or email, or import them from your contacts
    - **Upload a CSV** of names, emails and phone numbers (web only)

    Guests receive their invitation by email, SMS or WhatsApp, with a personal RSVP link. They don't need the app to respond. [Guests & RSVPs →](/private-events/guests-rsvp)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on the tools you need">
    Add only what your event needs. Popular first tools:

    - [Event Schedule](/private-events/event-schedule) so guests know the plan
    - [Food & Beverage Preferences](/private-events/food-beverage) to collect dietary needs
    - [Tasks](/private-events/tasks) to assign jobs to co-hosts, guests or vendors
    - [Photos](/private-events/photos) for a shared album on the day
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track responses">
    Open the [Event Dashboard](/private-events/event-dashboard) to see RSVPs as they arrive, and send a reminder to anyone who hasn't responded.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Prefer to type it than tap it? [Connect Fast Party to Claude or ChatGPT](/claude-integration/overview) and say something like: "Create a birthday dinner for Saturday at 7 pm at our house, invite my family circle, and set up a potluck."
</Tip>
