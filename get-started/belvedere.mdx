**Your space, beautifully run.** Belvedere is Fast Party set up for spaces that put on programs: museums, galleries, cultural centers, cafés, studios, halls and co-working spaces. It puts your program, your audience and the door on one platform that remembers every night you've run.

## Your workspace, in venue language

When your organization is set up as a **venue or space**, the workspace relabels itself:

| In Fast Party | In Belvedere |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Your venue |
| Circles | Audiences and member groups |
| Members | Visitors and members |
| Events | Programs |
| Managers | Program managers |

## Get set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your workspace with our team">
    Email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai) to create your organization. We set up your logo and brand, your own WhatsApp Business number and email sender, and ticket and membership revenue into your own Razorpay or Stripe account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring your audience across">
    Send us your ticketing exports, mailing lists and past program records. Our team imports them once, so you start knowing who has already come. [How data migration works →](/data-migration)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your audiences">
    Group people once, as members, regulars, patrons or a list per program, and they stay. Posts and polls keep them engaged between visits. [Circles →](/recurring-events/overview)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first program">
    Run an opening, a talk, a gig, a workshop or a pop-up market, free or ticketed. Use a [Registered Event](/public-events/overview) for tickets and sign-ups, with QR check-in at the door, or an Open Event for members-only programs where everyone in the group is welcome without an RSVP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the room">
    Send invitations to exactly the audiences you choose. Each visitor receives a message only once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Visitors don't need an app. Tickets, check-in and every visitor tool work in the browser from a WhatsApp or email link.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization admin** | Manage your audiences, broadcasts and settings. Only admins change who someone is. |
| **Program manager** | Run their own programs and audiences. |
| **Visitor or member** | Register, buy tickets, check in, and take part in posts and polls. |

## Ask your venue data anything

[Connect Claude or ChatGPT](/claude-integration/overview) to your workspace and ask in plain language:

- "Which program filled the room fastest?"
- "Who came to three openings but never joined?"
- "Draft the invitation for Friday's opening."
