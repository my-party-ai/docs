**Every event, start to standing ovation.** Jubila is Fast Party set up for event planners and agencies. It puts guests, tickets, schedules, vendors and the day itself on one platform that remembers every event you've run.

## Your workspace, in planner language

When your organization is set up as an **event planner or agency**, the workspace relabels itself:

| In Fast Party | In Jubila |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Your planning business |
| Circles | Guest lists and audiences, by client, city or tier |
| Members | Guests |
| Events | Events and shows |
| Managers | Event coordinators |

## Get set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your workspace with our team">
    Email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai) to create your organization. We set up your logo and brand, your own WhatsApp Business number and email sender, and ticket revenue into your own Razorpay or Stripe account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring your history across">
    Send us your client records, guest lists and past events from spreadsheets, ticketing exports or wherever they live. Our team imports them once, so you start with your history in place. [How data migration works →](/data-migration)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your guest lists">
    Group guests once, by client, city, tour or tier, and they stay. A guest joins your roster once and can be placed on any list without another import. [Circles →](/recurring-events/overview)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the event">
    Create a free or ticketed event for a client or for yourself. Use a [Registration Event](/public-events/overview) for registrations and tickets, or an [RSVP Event](/private-events/event-creation) for an invitation-only guest list. Each event has its own dashboard and tools, including [Event Schedule](/private-events/event-schedule) and [Event Lineup](/private-events/event-lineup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in your vendors">
    Add caterers, decorators and other vendors to any event. Once they accept, give them tasks and alert them to changes. They see only what's theirs. [Vendors →](/vendors/overview)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the day">
    Check attendees in by QR code, check vendors in on arrival, and collect feedback afterwards with [Feedback Corner](/private-events/feedback-corner).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization admin** | Manage your roster, all guest lists, broadcasts and settings. Only admins change who someone is. |
| **Event coordinator** | Run their own events and guest lists. |
| **Guest** | RSVP or register, pay for tickets, and use the event's tools. |

Ticket revenue and fees go straight to your own account. Fast Party never holds the money.

<Note>
  Your organization's brand appears on every page and message. Separate branding for each client's event is on the roadmap. Ask us where it stands.
</Note>
