## Your workspace, in planner language

When your organization is set up as an **event planner or agency**, the workspace relabels itself:

| In Fast Party | In Jubila |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Your planning business |
| Circles | Guest lists and audiences, by client, city or tier |
| Members | Guests |
| Events | Events and shows |
| Managers | Event coordinators |

## Get set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your workspace with our team">
    Email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai) to create your organization. We set up your logo and brand, and connect ticket revenue to your own Razorpay (India) or Stripe (US) account. [Set up your organization →](/organizations/setup)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp and email">
    We set up your own WhatsApp Business number and email sender through MSG91, so every guest hears from your business, not from Fast Party. Messaging is billed at cost to you: ₹0.15 per WhatsApp message and ₹0.04 per email. [Your WhatsApp & Email →](/notifications/your-channels)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring your history across">
    Send us your client records, guest lists and past events from spreadsheets, ticketing exports or wherever they live. We import them once, so you start with your history in place. [How data migration works →](/data-migration)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your AI">
    Click **Connect with Claude** in the top-right corner of any page, or set up ChatGPT. Your workspace is now ready to go. [Connect your AI →](/platforms#connect-your-ai)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in your coordinators">
    Invite the people who run events for you as managers. Each one runs only the guest lists and events they create or are assigned to. [Admins and managers →](/organizations/admins-and-managers)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Run a client event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the guest list">
    Create a guest list (a circle) for the client, the city or the tier, and add guests once. A guest joins your roster once and can be placed on any list without another import. [Guest lists →](/recurring-events/overview)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the event type">
    - An [**RSVP Event**](/private-events/event-creation) for an invitation-only guest list, such as a wedding or a private launch.
    - A [**Registration Event**](/public-events/overview) for sign-ups and tickets, with or without your approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it look like the client's event">
    Add a banner and an invitation template from the template store or the client's own design. A wedding with a Mehendi, Sangeet and reception can run as **one multi-day event with a different invitation for each day**, up to 5. [Banner and templates →](/events/banner-and-templates)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on the tools the event needs">
    Share the run-of-show with [Event Schedule](/private-events/event-schedule) and the performers with [Event Lineup](/private-events/event-lineup). Collect food choices with [Guest Preferences](/private-events/food-beverage) and flights and rooms with [Travel & Stay](/private-events/travel-stay). Assign jobs to your team with [Tasks](/private-events/tasks). [All event tools →](/events/event-tools)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it from the Event Dashboard">
    Watch RSVPs and registrations arrive, message every guest at once with an event broadcast, check people in at the door, and see what's outstanding. Afterwards, collect feedback with [Feedback Corner](/private-events/feedback-corner). [Event Dashboard →](/private-events/event-dashboard)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Destination weddings and multi-city tours are where Jubila earns its keep: one event across several days, Travel & Stay collecting every arrival, and Tasks keeping transfers, décor and catering on schedule.
</Tip>

## Keep guests informed

Guests don't need the app. Every invitation, confirmation, update and alert reaches them on WhatsApp and email, and in the app if they have it.

- **Post updates on the event wall** when something changes on the day. [The event wall →](/notifications/event-wall)
- **Send an event broadcast** from the Event Dashboard to reach every guest at once.
- **Send an organization broadcast** to your regulars across several guest lists, for example to announce your next show. [Broadcasts →](/organizations/broadcasts)

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization admin** | Everything: your roster, all guest lists and events, broadcasts and settings. |
| **Event coordinator** | Run the guest lists and events they create or are assigned to. |
| **Guest** | RSVP or register, pay for tickets, and use the event's tools. |

Ticket revenue and fees go straight to your own account. Fast Party never holds the money.

## Ask your event data anything

Once your workspace is [connected to Claude or ChatGPT](/claude-integration/overview), ask in plain language:

- "Which of this season's events sold out fastest?"
- "Who came to last year's New Year show but hasn't bought this year?"
- "List every guest arriving on Friday who still needs an airport pickup."
- "Draft the invitation for the Sangeet night."

Your AI sees only what your account can see in Fast Party. And [Ask Antsy](/features/antsy-ai), on every page of the app, guides your coordinators step by step.
