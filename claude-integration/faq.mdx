<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to install anything?">
    No. For Claude, log in to Fast Party and click **Connect with Claude** in the top-right corner of any page. For ChatGPT, add Fast Party once as a custom app using the server URL. See [Connect Claude](/claude-integration/connect) and [Connect ChatGPT](/chatgpt-integration/setup).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which plans do I need?">
    **Claude:** Pro or higher. Connectors aren't available on the free plan.

    **ChatGPT:** a paid plan, on the web, with developer mode turned on for custom apps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my Fast Party password shared with my AI?">
    No. You sign in to Fast Party through a secure authorization flow, the same kind used by "Sign in with Google." Your AI never sees your password. Fast Party issues a secure token with limited access to your account, which you can revoke at any time by disconnecting.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my AI see things I can't?">
    No. The connection uses your own Fast Party permissions. A manager connected to their AI sees and manages only what they can in Fast Party, and an admin sees what an admin can.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my AI delete my events or data?">
    It only does what you ask. If you ask it to delete an event, it can. If you don't, it won't. Actions that change things, such as sending invitations, ask for your confirmation first.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
    In Fast Party. When you ask your AI to do something, it sends the instruction to Fast Party, Fast Party carries it out, and the data stays in your Fast Party account. We never train AI models on your event data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does it work on mobile?">
    **Claude:** yes. Once connected, Fast Party works in all your Claude conversations, including the Claude mobile app.

    **ChatGPT:** set it up and use it on the web.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my whole team connect?">
    Yes. Each person connects their own Fast Party account to their own AI. Everyone works with their own events and data, within their own permissions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to reconnect every time?">
    No. Once connected, it stays connected. Occasionally, for example after a security reset, you may be asked to sign in again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if my AI says it can't access Fast Party?">
    Check the connection is still active in your AI's connector or app settings. If it's disconnected, connect it again. If it's connected, start a new conversation and ask again. Still stuck? Email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I disconnect?">
    Your AI loses access to your Fast Party account. Your events, guests and history stay exactly as they are, and you can reconnect at any time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
