Connect Fast Party to the AI you already use, and your whole workspace (events, guests, circles, organization and history) is available to it in plain language. Ask a question and get the answer from your own data. Ask for something to be done, and it's done in Fast Party while you watch.

## How it works

Fast Party runs an MCP server with **250+ tools**. MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a standard way for an app to let an AI assistant work with it. You don't need to know any more than that: connect once, then just ask.

Your data stays in Fast Party. Your AI works with it using your own permissions, so it can't see or do anything you can't.

## What you can do

| Area | For example |
| --- | --- |
| **Your organization** | "Which circles have been quiet this month?" "Invite Priya as a manager for the Hyderabad chapter." |
| **Circles** | "Create a circle for the 2015 batch." "Run a poll on the best date for the reunion." |
| **Events** | "Create a registration event for 300 people, with approval." "Change Saturday's start time to 7 pm." |
| **Event tools** | "Set up the schedule: welcome drinks at 7:30, dinner at 8, cake at 9:30." "Create a potluck with mains, salads and desserts." |
| **Guests and messages** | "Who hasn't responded yet? Send them a reminder." "Draft the invitation for the 2010 batch." |
| **Your history** | "Who has attended every annual gala for the last five years?" "What did the caterer quote last time?" |

You can chain requests too: one message can create the event, invite a circle and set the reminder. [See the full list →](/claude-integration/what-you-can-do)

## Your data, your control

- **Your permissions come with you.** Nobody gets more access through AI than they have in Fast Party.
- **You approve the work.** Review actions when asked, and disconnect at any time.
- **We never train AI models on your event data**, and never sell or share it for marketing.
