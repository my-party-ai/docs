Fast Party connects to Claude as a **custom MCP connector**. Once connected, you can create events, invite guests, manage agendas, and more — all through conversation with Claude, without opening the Fast Party app.

## What is this?

Fast Party connects to Claude through a technology called MCP (Model Context Protocol) — a standard way for apps to give Claude the ability to take actions on your behalf. Fast Party's MCP server is hosted at `mcp.fastparty.ai` and powers the integration automatically once you connect.

You don't need to understand any of that to use it. Connect once, and then just ask Claude to do things.

## Who is this for?

- **Existing Fast Party users** who also use Claude and want to manage everything from one place
- **Claude power users** who prefer doing everything through AI conversation
- **Teams using Claude for productivity** who want event planning built in alongside their other tools
