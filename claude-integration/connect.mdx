Fast Party connects to Claude as a **custom MCP connector** — not through the Connector Store. You add it once using the Fast Party MCP server URL, and then Fast Party is available in all your Claude conversations.

<Note>
  Fast Party in Claude requires a Claude Pro account or higher. The connection is per user — each person connects their own Fast Party account.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **The easy way:** log in to [app.fastparty.ai](https://app.fastparty.ai) and click the **Connect with Claude** button in the top-right corner of any page. Fast Party is connected to your Claude account in one click. Use the steps below only if you'd rather add it by hand.
</Tip>

## Steps to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Claude">
    Go to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) or open the Claude desktop app. In the left sidebar, click **Customize**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/claude-connect/image.png" alt="Claude home — click Customize in the left sidebar" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to Connectors">
    In the Customize panel, select **Connectors** from the left menu.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/claude-connect/image2.png" alt="Customize panel — Connectors section" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Fast Party as a custom connector">
    Click the **+** icon at the top of the Connectors panel, then select **Add custom connector**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/claude-connect/image3.png" alt="Click + then Add custom connector" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>

    In the dialog that appears, enter:
    - **Name:** `Fast Party`
    - **URL:** `https://mcp.fastparty.ai/mcp`

    Then click **Add**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/claude-connect/image4.png" alt="Add custom connector dialog with Fast Party name and MCP URL" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Fast Party will appear in your Connectors list tagged as **CUSTOM**. Click **Connect** on the Fast Party listing.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/claude-connect/image5.png" alt="Fast Party CUSTOM connector listed — click Connect" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in to Fast Party">
    A Fast Party sign-in window will open. Log in with your Fast Party account — using Google, Microsoft, phone number, or email. If you don't have an account yet, you can sign up for free at [app.fastparty.ai](https://app.fastparty.ai/).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/claude-connect/image6.png" alt="Sign in to Fast Party" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorise the connection">
    After signing in, you'll see a confirmation that Fast Party is connected. Click **Open Claude** to return to the app.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/claude-connect/image7.png" alt="Connected confirmation — click Open Claude" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="You're connected">
    Fast Party is now active in Claude. You'll see it listed under Connectors with its full set of tools. To confirm it's working, ask Claude:

    > "What Fast Party tools do you have access to?"

    Claude will list all the actions it can take on your behalf.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/claude-connect/image8.png" alt="Fast Party connected — tool permissions visible in Claude" style={{ borderRadius: "8px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What permissions are requested?

When you connect Fast Party to Claude, you're giving Claude the ability to:

- Create and update your events
- Add guests and send invitations
- View RSVP statuses
- Set up agendas, tasks, food preferences, and potlucks
- Create and manage guest circles

Fast Party only accesses your data when you ask Claude to do something. It doesn't run in the background or make changes without your instruction.

## Disconnecting

To remove the connection, go to **Customize → Connectors → Fast Party → Disconnect**.

Disconnecting removes Claude's access to your Fast Party account. Your Fast Party data is never affected — everything stays intact in Fast Party.
