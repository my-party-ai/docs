Connecting Fast Party to Claude takes about two minutes. You'll do it once, and then Fast Party is available in all your Claude conversations.

<Note>
  Fast Party in Claude requires a Claude Pro account or higher. The connection is per user — each person connects their own Fast Party account.
</Note>

## What permissions are requested?

When you connect Fast Party to Claude, you're giving Claude the ability to:

- Create and update your events
- Add guests and send invitations
- View RSVP statuses
- Set up agendas, tasks, food preferences, and potlucks
- Create and manage guest circles

Fast Party only accesses your data when you ask Claude to do something. It doesn't run in the background or make changes without your instruction.

## Disconnecting

To remove the connection, go to **Claude Settings → Connectors → Fast Party → Disconnect**.

Disconnecting removes Claude's access to your Fast Party account. Your Fast Party data is never affected — everything stays intact in Fast Party.
