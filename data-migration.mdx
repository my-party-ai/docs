Your history is probably scattered: member and alumni lists in spreadsheets, contacts in phones, updates in WhatsApp groups, photos across folders and notes on paper. Data Migration brings it into your organization in one move, so you start with your history in place instead of rebuilding it by hand.

<Note>
  Data Migration is a **white-glove, one-time service** carried out with you by the Fast Party team. It isn't a self-serve upload.
</Note>

## What we bring across

| For | We typically migrate |
| --- | --- |
| **Alumni associations** | Alumni records, batch and chapter lists, past reunions, who came and who contributed |
| **Clubs and associations** | Member register, sections, past events, attendance and collections |
| **Planners and agencies** | Client records, guest lists, ticketing history, past events and vendors |
| **Venues and spaces** | Members, patrons, past programs, who came and what they bought |

We work from wherever your data lives today: spreadsheets, ticketing exports, WhatsApp, documents, photo folders and paper.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get in touch">
    Email us the details listed below: how much history you have and where it lives.
  </Step>

  <Step title="We scope it with you">
    Our team reviews what you have and agrees the plan with you: what's moving, from where, and how it will be organized into your circles and events.
  </Step>

  <Step title="We do the migration">
    We carry out the one-time import for you. It takes about a month, depending on how much data you have and what shape it's in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Your history is live">
    Your records land organized in your organization, and your connected AI can answer questions about them from day one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Where people share a phone number, such as family members, each person keeps their own record. Anything that looks like a duplicate is checked by a person rather than merged automatically.
