## What's included

- **Historical event data migration** — past events, dates, and event details imported into your Fast Party account
- **Guest lists** — existing guest and contact data migrated across
- **Event history** — prior event records made available in your account
- **Optional Notion integration** — if you manage events or operations in Notion, we can set up a custom Notion workspace with Fast Party MCP tools so you can manage Fast Party directly from Notion

## Who it's for

The Data Migration Service is for teams and individuals who:

- Are switching to Fast Party from another platform or spreadsheet-based workflow
- Have years of event history they want to preserve
- Use Notion for operations and want Fast Party embedded in their existing workspace
