Travel & Stay lets attendees share their travel logistics — arrival time, accommodation details, and departure — so you can coordinate transport, room assignments, and departures without chasing people individually.

<Note>
  Travel & Stay is most useful for destination conferences, retreats, and multi-day events where attendees are travelling from out of town or staying overnight.
</Note>

## How attendees submit their details

Attendees open the **Travel & Stay** section from their ticket link and complete a 4-step form:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Arrival">
    - Arrival date and time
    - Luggage count: No Bags, 1–2 bags, 3–4 bags, or 5+ bags
  </Step>
  <Step title="Room">
    - Bed configuration preference: King Bed, Twin Beds, or Double + Single
    - Check-in date and time
    - Check-out date and time
  </Step>
  <Step title="Departure">
    - Departure date and time
    - Luggage count (with a souvenir variant for attendees picking up event materials)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Attendees review their submitted details and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Host management view

As the host, you see all submitted travel and stay details in one consolidated view:

- Who is arriving and when
- Luggage counts (useful for coordinating shuttles or porters)
- Room preferences and check-in/check-out windows
- Departure times

<Tip>
  Share the Travel & Stay link with attendees several weeks before the event so you have time to arrange transport, confirm room assignments, and brief your logistics team.
</Tip>
