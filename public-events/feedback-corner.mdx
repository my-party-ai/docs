The Feedback Corner is a post-event feature that lets you collect structured feedback from attendees after the event — ratings, comments, and overall impressions in one place.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Host enables Feedback Corner">
    After the event, the host turns on the Feedback Corner from the event settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attendees receive a feedback request">
    Confirmed registrants are notified and invited to submit feedback via their ticket link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Host reviews results">
    The host sees aggregate ratings and all written comments in one dashboard view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What attendees can submit

- An overall event rating
- Written comments or suggestions

## What hosts see

- Aggregate rating across all responses
- Individual written comments from attendees
- Response count

<Tip>
  Use Feedback Corner after conferences, corporate events, and retreats to gather structured input you can act on for the next edition.
</Tip>
