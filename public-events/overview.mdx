A **Registration Event** is for events where people sign up through a link rather than receiving a personal invitation: conferences, reunions, festivals, tournaments, workshops, fundraisers and ticketed shows. The people who register are your **attendees**.

On a Registration Event, the **Registration** button takes the place of **Invite**. It opens your registration setup, tiers and attendee list.

## Approval or direct registration

When you set up registration, you choose how it works:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Approval required" icon="user-check">
    People request a spot and answer your question, and you approve or reject each one. If the event is paid, attendees pay once you've approved them. Best when you want to vet who comes.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Direct registration" icon="bolt">
    People register straight away, with no approval step. Best when anyone with the link is welcome.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Setting it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the event">
    Click **Create Event**, choose **Registration Event**, and add the name, dates and venue, just as you would for any event. Add a [banner and event template](/events/banner-and-templates): they're the first thing people see on your registration page. Multi-day events can have a template for each day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up registration">
    Open **Registration** and click the pencil on **Settings**. Choose approval or direct registration, the question for registrants, when registration closes, any age restriction, and whether the event is free or paid. Your event can't be shared until this is done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add tiers">
    Create one or more tiers, such as a 3-day pass, a day pass and a family pass, each with its own capacity and badge color, and a price if the event is paid. Paid events use your own Razorpay (India) or Stripe (US) account. Fast Party never holds the money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    Click **Share Invite** and share the registration link on WhatsApp, email, your website or social media. You can also **Add Attendees** yourself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing registrations

The Registration page counts everyone who's registered, waiting for review, approved and rejected, and shows how much capacity is used.

- **Approve** or **Reject** requests one at a time, or **Approve All** at once
- **Pause Registration** to stop new sign-ups for a while, and resume when you're ready
- See where each attendee came from: your link, or your own invitation

Like every event, you set it up on the Event Page and run it from the [Event Dashboard](/private-events/event-dashboard), which shows registrations by tier, revenue and check-ins.

## On the day: QR check-in

Every confirmed attendee gets a ticket with a QR code. At the door, you or your team scan it to check them in. If someone can't find theirs, check them in by hand from the attendee list.

This is different from [Check-In](/private-events/self-check-in) at an RSVP Event, where guests check themselves in.
