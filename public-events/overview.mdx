A **Registration Event** is for events where people sign up through a link rather than receiving a personal invitation: conferences, reunions, tournaments, workshops, fundraisers and ticketed shows. The people who register are your **attendees**.

## Approval or direct registration

When you create a Registration Event, you choose how registrations work:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Approval required" icon="user-check">
    People request a spot and you approve or decline each one. If the event is priced, attendees pay once you've approved them. Best when you want to vet who comes.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Direct registration" icon="bolt">
    People register straight away, with no approval step. Best when anyone with the link is welcome.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Setting it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the event">
    Click **New Event**, choose **Registration Event**, and add the name, dates and venue, just as you would for any event. Add a [banner and event template](/events/banner-and-templates): they're the first thing people see on your registration page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up registration">
    In the event's settings, choose approval or direct registration and set:

    - **Capacity:** the most people who can register
    - **Registration end date:** when sign-ups close
    - **Branding:** the logo and name shown on the registration page
    - **Age restriction**, if the event needs one

    Your event can't be shared until registration setup is finished.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add tickets">
    Create one or more ticket types, free or paid, each with its own price, capacity and badge color. For paid tickets, link your payment account: Razorpay in India or Stripe in the US. Payments go straight to you. Fast Party never holds the money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open registration and share the link">
    Open registration and share the link on WhatsApp, email, your website or social media.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing registrations

The **registration dashboard** shows total registrations, revenue and a breakdown by ticket type, so you can see how the event is filling up.

- **Approve or decline** requests one at a time, or approve all pending requests at once (approval mode)
- **Pause registration** to stop new sign-ups for a while, and **resume** it when you're ready
- Keep an eye on capacity as tickets sell

Like every event, you set it up on the Event Page and run it from the [Event Dashboard](/private-events/event-dashboard).

## On the day: QR check-in

Every confirmed attendee gets a ticket with a QR code. At the door, you or your team scan it to check them in, and the dashboard shows who has arrived.

This is different from [Self Check-In](/private-events/self-check-in) at an RSVP Event, where guests mark themselves as arrived.
