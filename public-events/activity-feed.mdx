The Event Activity Feed is a real-time group chat shared by all event participants — hosts, co-hosts, and confirmed registrants. It keeps everyone connected before, during, and after the event.

## What you can do

- **Post messages** — write a message and share it with everyone on the event.
- **Attach media** — attach images or PDF files to your post.
- **Share GIFs** — pick a GIF from the built-in GIF picker.
- **Reply to messages** — reply to any post to keep conversations threaded.
- **React with emoji** — add emoji reactions to any message.
- **Delete or edit your own messages** — update or remove anything you've posted.

## Who can post

All confirmed event participants can post in the Activity Feed — hosts, co-hosts, and approved registrants alike.

## Content moderation

The Activity Feed has a built-in content filter. Inappropriate language is blocked before it's posted, keeping the feed appropriate for all attendees.

## System messages

Alongside user posts, the feed includes automatic system messages that record key event activity — for example, when a registrant is approved or when the agenda is published. You can filter to show only system messages for a clean event log.

<Tip>
  Use the Activity Feed in the days leading up to your event to share reminders, logistics updates, and answers to common questions — reaching all confirmed attendees at once.
</Tip>
