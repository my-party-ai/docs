The Food & Beverage feature lets you find out what your attendees can and can't eat before the event — so you can plan catering quantities and menu options accurately.

<Note>
  Food preference data is shown only to you as the event organiser. Attendees only see the options, not each other's selections.
</Note>

## Setting up preferences

Go to the **Food & Beverage** tab of your event and add the dietary options relevant to your catering plan. Common options include:

- No restrictions
- Vegetarian
- Vegan
- Gluten-free
- Dairy-free
- Nut allergy
- Halal
- Kosher

You can add custom options for any event — for example, "No seafood" or "Low-sodium."

## Enabling registrant responses

Preferences are hidden from registrants until you enable them. Once enabled, registrants see the options on their ticket page and can select whatever applies to them.

You can enable and disable responses at any time without losing the data already collected.

## Viewing responses

From the **Food & Beverage** tab, you can:

- See a summary count for each preference option (e.g. "12 guests selected Vegetarian")
- View individual registrant selections
- Export the full list as a spreadsheet — useful for sharing with your caterer

<Tip>
  Ask Antsy to set things up: "Set up vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and nut-allergy options and enable attendees to select them."
</Tip>
