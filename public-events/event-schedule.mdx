## Creating activities

Go to the **Agenda** tab of your event and click **Add activity**. Each activity has:

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Title** | What's happening — e.g. "Registration opens", "Keynote", "Networking lunch" |
| **Start time** | When the activity begins |
| **End time** | When the activity finishes |
| **Notes** | Optional description or speaker notes |
| **Location or room** | Optional — useful for multi-track or multi-room events |
| **Links** | Optional. Attach a URL and label (e.g. a slide deck, a room map) |
| **File attachments** | Optional. Attach up to 5 files per activity |

Activities are sorted automatically by start time. Overlapping activities are flagged with a warning.

## Attaching files to activities

Attach up to **5 files** per activity — PDFs, spreadsheets, Word documents, images, and other common types. Useful for sharing presentation decks, briefings, or reference materials with attendees alongside a specific session.

## Publishing the schedule

Your schedule is private until you publish it. Once published, confirmed registrants can view the full programme from their ticket link — no account required.

Updates to a published schedule appear immediately — no need to re-publish after edits.

## Exporting as PDF

Export the full schedule as a PDF. Useful for printing, sharing with speakers, or distributing offline.

## Unpublishing

You can unpublish the schedule at any time. Registrants can no longer see it, but your data is saved and can be republished later.

## Attendee check-in from the schedule

Hosts can check in registrants directly from the agenda page. See [Registrant Check-In](/public-events/registrant-check-in) for the full guide.
