## Creating tasks

Go to the **Tasks** tab of your event and click **Add task**. Each task has:

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Title** | What needs to be done — e.g. "Confirm AV setup", "Send speaker briefing" |
| **Due date** | When it needs to be completed |
| **Assigned to** | Yourself or a co-host (optional) |
| **Notes** | Any extra context or instructions |

You can also ask Antsy to create tasks for you:

> "Remind me to confirm the venue layout by the 5th, send speaker briefs by the 8th, and finalise catering numbers by the 10th."

Antsy creates all three tasks at once.

## Task status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Open** | Not yet started |
| **In progress** | Being worked on |
| **Complete** | Done |

## Assigning tasks

Tasks can be assigned to yourself or any co-host on the event. When you assign a task to someone else, they receive an email notification with the task details and due date.

## Tracking progress

The Tasks view shows all tasks sorted by due date. Filter by status or assignee to focus on what matters right now. Overdue tasks are highlighted so they're easy to spot.
