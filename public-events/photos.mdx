## Photo albums

Photos are organised into albums. Browse by album type:

| Album type | What's here |
|------------|-------------|
| **My Albums** | Albums you've created or uploaded to |
| **Host Albums** | Albums created by the event host |
| **Guest Albums** | Albums contributed by attendees |
| **Favorites** | Photos you've marked as a favourite |
| **Deleted** | Recently deleted photos (trash) |

## Viewing photos

Open your event and go to the **Photos** tab to see all photos for that event. Photos from the host and attendees are shown together, and you can filter by album type.

## Adding photos

Tap **Add Photos** and select images from your device. Your photos are added to the event album and visible to other attendees.

## Favouriting photos

Tap the heart icon on any photo to add it to your **Favorites** album. Favourites are personal — others don't see which photos you've favourited.

## Deleting photos

You can delete photos you've uploaded. Deleted photos move to the **Deleted** album and can be recovered for a limited period before permanent removal.

<Note>
  You can only delete photos that you uploaded. Hosts can manage photos across the full event album.
</Note>

## Per-event albums

Every event automatically gets its own album. After the event, the album stays accessible so you and your attendees can revisit the memories at any time.
