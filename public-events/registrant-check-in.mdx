At a Registered Event, check-in is tied to registration. Every confirmed attendee has a ticket with a unique QR code, and only confirmed attendees can be checked in.

## Who can be checked in

Only attendees with a confirmed registration and a valid ticket can be checked in. Pending or declined registrations don't appear as eligible.

## QR code check-in

The fastest way to handle a busy entrance:

- Each attendee's ticket carries a unique QR code, in the app and in their confirmation message.
- At the door, you or your team scan the code.
- The attendee is checked in instantly, and the check-in view updates for everyone on your team.

<Tip>
  For conferences or large events, run several check-in points on different phones or tablets to handle peak arrival times.
</Tip>

## Check someone in by hand

If an attendee can't find their QR code:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the check-in view">
    Open the event's **Guest Check-In** view. You can also reach it from the Event Schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the attendee">
    Search by name or scroll the list. Each name shows the attendee's ticket type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark them as arrived">
    Tap the check-in button next to their name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Spot ticket types at a glance

The check-in view shows each attendee's ticket type and its badge color, so your team can tell General Admission from VIP, or any other ticket type you've set up, at a glance.

<Note>
  This is different from [Self Check-In](/private-events/self-check-in) at an RSVP Event, where guests mark themselves as arrived.
</Note>
