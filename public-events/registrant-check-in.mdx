For Registered Events, check-in integrates directly with the registration system. Only approved registrants with a valid ticket can be checked in.

## How check-in works for registered events

Every approved registrant has a ticket with a unique QR code. At check-in, the QR code is scanned (or the host finds the registrant in the list) to confirm their arrival.

Only guests who have been **approved** and have a valid registration order can be checked in. Pending or declined registrants do not appear as eligible for check-in.

## Host manual check-in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Guest Check-In">
    Go to your event and open the **Guest Check-In** view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the registrant">
    Search by name or scroll the list. Registration details and ticket type are shown alongside each name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as arrived">
    Tap the check-in button next to their name to confirm their arrival.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list updates in real time so you always have an accurate count of who's on-site.

## QR code check-in

The fastest check-in method for events with large volumes of arrivals:

- Each registrant's ticket includes a unique QR code.
- Display a scanner at the entrance — on a tablet or screen.
- Registrants present their QR code (from the app or email) and scan to check in instantly.
- The host's check-in view updates automatically.

<Tip>
  For conferences or large events, set up multiple check-in stations using different devices to handle peak arrival times smoothly.
</Tip>

## Self-check-in

Registrants can also self-check-in on arrival by tapping **Check In** from their ticket view. This is useful for lower-traffic events or when you want to reduce door staffing.

## Verifying ticket types

The check-in view shows each registrant's ticket type alongside their name. Use the badge colour assigned to each ticket type to quickly identify General Admission vs. VIP attendees, or any other distinction you've set up.

## Check-in from the agenda

The Guest Check-In view is also accessible from the **Agenda** page — useful if you're managing both the schedule and arrivals from the same screen during the event.
