Registration Management is the control center for your Registered Event — configure tickets, set capacity, manage branding, and approve or decline every registration request.

## Registration setup

Before opening registration, configure your registration settings:

### Capacity

Choose the maximum number of attendees:

| Option | |
|--------|--|
| 50 | 100 |
| 250 | 500 |
| 1,000 | 1,000+ |

### Registration end date

Set a date after which guests can no longer submit registration requests. This lets you close registrations before the event to finalise your headcount.

### Branding

- **Brand logo** — upload your organisation's logo to appear on the registration page.
- **Brand name** — the name shown as the organiser on registration communications.

### Age restriction

Enable the **18+ restriction** to require guests to confirm they are 18 or over when registering.

### Payment profile

If you're selling paid tickets, link a payment profile to collect revenue from registrants. Set this up under your account settings before creating paid tickets.

## Ticket types

Create multiple ticket types for a single event — for example, General Admission and VIP, or Early Bird and Standard.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Label** | The ticket name shown to guests — e.g. "General Admission", "VIP" |
| **Price** | Free or Paid. For paid tickets, enter the price and currency (INR). |
| **Capacity** | How many tickets of this type are available |
| **Notes** | Optional notes shown to guests — e.g. "Includes dinner" |
| **Badge colour** | A colour tag used in the registration dashboard to identify ticket holders at a glance |

## Opening and pausing registration

Once configured, share the registration link to open registrations. You can also **pause** registration at any time — for example, while reviewing a batch of requests — and resume it when ready.

## Registration dashboard

The registration dashboard gives you a live overview:

- Total registrations received
- Approved, declined, and pending counts
- Revenue collected (for paid tickets)
- Breakdown by ticket type

## Managing registrants

The **Registrants** tab shows every registration request with their name, ticket type, payment status, and registration date.

**Available actions:**

| Action | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Approve** | Confirms the guest — they're notified and added to the guest list |
| **Decline** | Rejects the request — the guest is notified |
| **Bulk approve pending** | Approves all pending requests at once |
| **Bulk approve all** | Approves every registration regardless of current status |
| **Bulk reject** | Declines multiple registrations at once |

## Guest ticket view

Once approved, each registrant receives a confirmation with their ticket details. Their ticket includes a QR code used for check-in at the event. See [Registrant Check-In](/public-events/registrant-check-in) for details.

<Note>
  Guests on Registered Events do not receive direct invitations. They find the event through the registration link you share and submit their own request. Once approved, they receive confirmation and can access the full event details.
</Note>
