## Registration setup

Set these up in the event's settings before you share the event. Your event can't be shared until registration setup is finished.

### Approval or direct registration

| Mode | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Approval required** | People request a spot, and you approve or decline each request. If the event is priced, attendees pay once you've approved them. |
| **Direct registration** | People register straight away, with no approval step. |

### Capacity

Choose the most people who can register: 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000 or 1,000+.

### Registration end date

Set a date after which no more registrations are accepted, so you can finalize your headcount before the event.

### Branding

- **Brand logo:** shown on the registration page
- **Brand name:** the name shown as the organizer on registration messages

### Age restriction

Turn on the **18+ restriction** to ask attendees to confirm they're 18 or over when they register.

### Payment account

To sell paid tickets, link your payment account first: Razorpay in India or Stripe in the US. Ticket revenue goes straight to you. Fast Party never holds the money. Organizations set this up once in [organization setup](/organizations/setup#payments).

## Ticket types

Create as many ticket types as you need, such as General Admission and VIP, or Early Bird and Standard.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Label** | The ticket name attendees see, such as "General Admission" |
| **Price** | Free or paid. For paid tickets, enter the price. |
| **Capacity** | How many tickets of this type are available |
| **Notes** | Optional notes shown to attendees, such as "Includes dinner" |
| **Badge color** | A color used to spot this ticket type at a glance on the dashboard and at check-in |

## Opening and pausing registration

Share the registration link to open registration. You can **pause** registration at any time, for example while you review a batch of requests, and **resume** it when you're ready.

## Registration dashboard

The registration dashboard gives you a live overview:

- Total registrations received
- Approved, declined and pending counts
- Revenue collected for paid tickets
- A breakdown by ticket type

## Managing attendees

The attendee list shows every registration with the person's name, ticket type, payment status and registration date.

| Action | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Approve** | Confirms the attendee. They're notified and receive their ticket. |
| **Decline** | Declines the request. The person is notified. |
| **Approve all pending** | Approves every pending request at once |
| **Decline in bulk** | Declines several requests at once |

Approve and decline apply when the event uses approval. With direct registration, attendees are confirmed as soon as they register.

## Attendee tickets

Every confirmed attendee receives a ticket with a QR code, which is scanned at the door. See [Attendee Check-In](/public-events/registrant-check-in).

<Note>
  Attendees at a Registered Event don't receive personal invitations. They find the event through the registration link you share and register themselves.
</Note>
