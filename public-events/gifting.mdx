## Enabling gifts

Gifts are off by default. To turn them on, open your event, go to **Gifting**, and toggle gifts on. Once enabled, confirmed registrants will see the gifting options from their ticket view.

## Digital Gifting

Digital Gifting lets attendees send cash gifts electronically. Three methods are supported:

**UPI QR code**
Upload your UPI QR code so attendees can scan it and pay directly through their preferred UPI app.

**Payment screenshot**
Attendees can complete a transfer via any method and upload a screenshot as proof of payment. You'll see the screenshots in the gifting dashboard.

**Gift cards**
Attendees can send digital gift cards, which are collected and displayed in your gifting summary.

All received digital gifts appear in the **Gifting Summary** view.

## Best Wishes

Best Wishes lets attendees send a personal message attached to your event.

**For attendees:**
- Write a message up to **2,000 characters**.
- Optionally attach up to **2 media files** (photos or videos).
- Submit from their ticket link.

**For hosts:**
- Customise the **banner text** shown on the Best Wishes page.
- View all submitted wishes in one place from the event dashboard.

## Disabling gifts

You can toggle gifting off at any time. Existing submissions are retained for you to view, but new submissions are no longer accepted.
