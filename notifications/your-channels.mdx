Open **Settings › Messaging** on the Admin Dashboard. Members hear from your organization's own WhatsApp number and email address, never from Fast Party, and the Fast Party team connects these with you. Invitations, updates and broadcasts all go out **in your name**. Only admins see this tab.

## Settings › Messaging

| On the tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp Business** | **Request setup** to start. It then shows **Request pending** until our team has connected your number. Templates need Meta's approval, which can take a few days. |
| **Email sender** | **Request setup** to start, then **Request pending** until your sending address is connected. Email templates need approval too. |
| **Cost** | What each message costs your organization. [What it costs ↓](#what-it-costs) |

## Why your own channels

- **Members recognize you.** A message from your alumni association or club gets opened. One from an unfamiliar sender often doesn't.
- **Replies come to you.** Members answer the organization they know.
- **Your brand, every time.** Your name on every message.

<Warning>
  Set up your channels before you add members, so their first message comes from your organization.
</Warning>

## How setup works

You don't have to work it out yourself: **our team sets it up with you.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request setup">
    On the Admin Dashboard, open **Settings › Messaging**. Tap **Request setup** for **WhatsApp Business** and for **Email sender**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We set up your messaging account">
    We help you create a messaging account in your organization's name. Your organization owns it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp Business number">
    We register your number with WhatsApp and get the Fast Party message templates approved for it. Approval can take a few days, so start early.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your email sender">
    We verify your sending domain, so emails arrive from your own address and don't land in spam.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    From then on, every message to your members goes out from your WhatsApp number and email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it costs

Messaging is billed to your organization at cost. Fast Party adds no markup.

| Channel | Cost per message |
| --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp** | ₹0.15 |
| **Email** | ₹0.04 |

Push and in-app notifications carry no messaging charge.

<Tip>
  **Example:** a broadcast to a 500-member circle that goes out on WhatsApp and email costs about ₹75 + ₹20 = **₹95**.
</Tip>

## The contact details on your organization page

The WhatsApp number and email you show on your [organization page](/organizations/org-page) are how visitors contact you. They're set in **Settings › Org page** and are separate from your messaging channels.
