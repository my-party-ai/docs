Your organization's WhatsApp number and email address become the way your members hear from you. Invitations, confirmations, updates, alerts and broadcasts all go out **in your name**, not Fast Party's.

## Why use your own channels

- **Members recognize you.** A message from your alumni association or club gets opened. One from an unfamiliar sender often doesn't.
- **Replies come to you.** Members answer the organization they know.
- **Your brand, every time.** Your name and logo on every message.

<Warning>
  If your own channels aren't set up, messages still go out, but from **Fast Party's default account**. Your members will see Fast Party as the sender, not your organization. We strongly recommend connecting your own.
</Warning>

## How setup works

Your WhatsApp and email run through **MSG91**, a messaging provider. You don't have to work it out yourself: **our team takes care of the setup with you.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell us you're ready">
    Email [contact@fastparty.ai](mailto:contact@fastparty.ai), or raise it during your organization onboarding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your MSG91 account">
    We help you create an MSG91 account in your organization's name. Your organization owns it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp Business number">
    We register your number with WhatsApp and get the Fast Party message templates approved for it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your email sender">
    We verify your sending domain, so emails arrive from your own address and don't land in spam.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    From then on, every message to your members goes out from your WhatsApp number and email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it costs

Messaging on your own channels is billed **separately** and paid by your organization, directly to MSG91. Fast Party adds no markup.

| Channel | Cost per message |
| --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp** | ₹0.15 |
| **Email** | ₹0.04 |

Push and in-app notifications carry no messaging charge.

<Tip>
  **Example:** an invitation to a 500-member circle, sent on WhatsApp and email, costs about ₹75 + ₹20 = **₹95**.
</Tip>
